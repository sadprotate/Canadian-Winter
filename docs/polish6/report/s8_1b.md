carries out the owner's decision on §8.1's open item (option C of `report/s8_1.md`, *Decision
needed*), and the owner's second decision on the grind_bench item (bare hands 30 % faster, *The
owner's second decision*). Every number is from this PC (headless, `--fixed-fps 60`).

The builds compared:

## What changed

**`ToolController` (hands and shovel-type tools)**
- A press scoops at once, during the recovery too. `_press_wait` is gone.
- **The waiting throw** (`throw_waiting`, public). A release after `min_scoop_hold_s` (counted from
  the press, as in §8.1) with a load:
  - outside the recovery, throws at once, as before;
  - inside it, sets `throw_waiting`. The throw goes on the first frame the recovery has run out,
    through `_release_throw(true)`, where the release aimed (second review fix: the release stores
    its facing, aim and distance; it used to go where the aim was then). Meanwhile the landing ring
    stays on that spot, recomputed every 0.06 s only for where the player stands. If the throw
    couldn't go there (nowhere to land, or indoors), the release is refused at once, as any release
    is, and no throw waits. The release also clears the fill's "Full — release to throw." hint.
  - A press before then cancels it. The load stays, kept, and the press goes on filling it as a
    stroke on a kept load (no footprint share, re-cut memory kept). The next release throws, or
    waits again if the recovery still runs. A press the cap refuses is ignored and cancels nothing
    (after review).
- **Interrupted, nothing is lost** ("the load stays, as with a kept load now"):
  - a tool switch drops the load at your feet, as it drops any load (`dump_at_feet`);
  - off foot (a vehicle, a blower, or the player frozen by a screen) the wait is cancelled and the
    load stays in the tool, kept; the next release on foot throws it;
  - with the mouse freed by a screen (`ui_block`), the same;
  - saving drops it at your feet, as it drops any load (`collect_state` → `dump_at_feet`);
  - indoors when it comes due, it doesn't go and the load stays, kept, without a sound or hint
    (after review; it used to play the error and "You can't throw snow from inside"); the next
    release outside throws it;
  - the pause menu pauses the tree, so the wait resumes when the game does.
- **A throw renews the bites:** `_release_throw()` clears `_cut_time` (after review; it was cleared
  at a fresh load's first stroke). See *Readings taken*.
- **After review:** a press the cap refuses doesn't cancel a waiting throw; a waiting throw that
  comes due indoors is kept without an error sound or hint (*After review*).
- **After the second review:** a waiting throw lands where its release aimed and its ring stays
  there (`_wait_fwd`, `_wait_aim`, `_wait_dist`, `_waiting_landing()`, `_release_throw(waited)`;
  `_hand_pos()` takes the facing and aim to throw from); a release inside the recovery that couldn't
  throw there is refused at once; a waiting release clears the fill's hint (`_full_hint()`)
  (*After the second review*).
- **The follow-through gives way to a scoop.** Scooping during it, the scoop's pose takes over once
  round 5's toss (`TOSS_MIN_S`, 0.42 s) has played since the throw. Not scooping, the
  follow-through still plays out over the recovery, as in §8.1. A tool switch doesn't bring back a
  follow-through the scoop ended (`_toss_cut`).

**Unchanged:** `throw_recovery_s` and `min_scoop_hold_s`, the press cap, the dig cycle, the footprint
fallback and its cap, the electric shovel's chute beat and the blowers' interval, push, throw
distances, the landing, poses and grip points, capacities and fill rates but one. No balance or save
change.

**The owner's second decision:** bare hands' fill rate ×1.3, 0.085 → 0.1105 m³/s
(`data/tools/hands.tres`, the generator's `_tools()`), the one data change. The ladder that already
exists (`ItemUpgrades._ladder_max`) then sells one of bare hands' two capacity levels; no code changed
for it. A comment in `balance_data.gd` (on `upgrade_hands_capacity_step`) says so; no balance value
changed.

**Benches** (they run every build; the new behaviour is keyed on `"throw_waiting" in tc`):
- grind_bench's and contract_bench's bots no longer hold through the recovery before sweeping. After
  the release they stay turned to the throw until a waiting throw has gone, as a player watches the
  ring. In a §8.1 build they behave as before.
- click_bench's in-place bots wait for a waiting throw before pressing again (a press would cancel
  it). Its human-rhythm bot doesn't look at anything: it keeps its fixed rhythm, so a press that
  comes before a waiting throw goes cancels it, as a hurried player's would.
- `shot.gd --scoop=thrown --repress=R [--rethrow=H]` for this section's shots.
- After the second review: `click_bench --rows=rhythm --turnback=N`, the review's experiment (the
  rhythm bot turns back to the snow N frames after its release and presses at the end of the gap),
  with two counts per row: strokes whose throw still waited at the press, and throws that landed
  back in the box. The rhythm rows' labels print two decimals (0.45 s, 0.25 s).

## Readings taken

- **The rule as written stalls the grind bot; a throw renews the re-cut memory** (for the
  coordinator to accept; the first version's reading, a fresh load, is below, and *After review*
  says why it moved to the throw).
  - First implementation, the rule exactly as set out (re-cut memory kept across every press):
    grind_bench `--full=1` removed **0.0040 m³/s** (0.48 m³ in 2 min), and its stall check
    stopped it.
  - Why: the bot turns, throws and turns back in about 8 frames, and now scoops at once. The cells
    it just cut were cut less than `RECUT_S` (0.3 s) ago, so their bite was used up. Each sweep
    frame over a cell refreshes its time, so while the sweep goes back and forth over them they
    never renew. §8.1's recovery used to keep the next press 0.5 s away, past `RECUT_S` (§8.1's
    binding note "keep recoveries above RECUT_S" and its `--recovery=0` stall are the same thing).
    A player who scoops again at once over the same patch would get a dead stroke too.
  - The reading that changes the least: **the first stroke of a fresh load (an empty tool) cuts with
    fresh bites.** It is §8.1's own rule for the footprint share ("only for a load's first stroke,
    so a click can't renew it"), on the same condition.
    - A fresh load follows a throw (at most one per recovery) or a dropped load.
    - A press that goes on filling a kept load keeps the memory: a short click, a cancelled waiting
      throw. So clicking still can't renew a bite. The click rows show it (0 throws, unchanged).
    - It renews no more often than §8.1 did: there, the recovery (0.5 s or more) let every bite
      renew before the next scoop could start.
  - With it: 0.0331 m³/s, and the throw rhythm costs the bot nothing (below).
  - **After review it goes with the throw instead** (`_release_throw()`), not with a fresh load's
    first stroke. Every bot empties the tool only by throwing, and nothing is cut between a throw
    and the next press, so every bench number is the same. What changes: no press ever clears the
    memory (§8.1's "isn't wiped by a new press", literally), and a load emptied another way
    (dropped at your feet: a tool switch, saving, the rack) keeps it, as in §8.1. So any later way
    of emptying the tool renews nothing; only a throw does, at most once a `throw_recovery_s`.
- **Where a waiting throw lands:** where the release aimed, and its ring shows that spot all along
  (second review fix; it first went where the aim was when it went). The release fixes the facing,
  the aim and the distance its pitch gives; the landing is worked out from where the player stands,
  so a player who walks during the wait carries the spot along with them, and the ring shows it.
- **A press refused by the cap** (over 6 a second) is ignored (after review): it doesn't scoop, and
  it doesn't cancel a waiting throw, which goes when the recovery ends. It used to cancel it, leaving
  the load kept with no scoop.
- **A waiting throw that comes due indoors** stays in the tool, kept, without a sound or hint (after
  review; nothing was pressed since the release, so §3's "You can't throw snow from inside" and the
  error sound came out of nowhere). The next release outside throws it.
- **A release inside the recovery with nowhere to land, or indoors,** is refused at once, as a
  release outside the recovery is ("Nowhere for the snow to land — keep holding it and aim
  somewhere else" or "You can't throw snow from inside.", the error sound), and the load stays; no
  throw waits (second review fix: with the landing fixed at the release, aiming elsewhere during the
  wait couldn't help, so the refusal comes at the release instead of when the recovery ends). A
  waiting throw that comes due with nowhere to land (the player walked) is still refused then.
- **The follow-through and the scoop** (a visual reading). With the scoop free during the recovery,
  §8.1's follow-through (as long as the recovery, up to 1 s for the sleigh) would have shown the
  tool up in the air while snow came into it. Round 5's toss (0.42 s) also overlapped a quick
  press's scoop. So a scoop takes over from the follow-through once round 5's toss has played.
  Shots below.
- **§8.1's `min_scoop_hold_s` counts from the press,** as in §8.1. A press now always starts its
  scoop at once, so the press and the scoop's start are the same (§8.1's option D is moot).
