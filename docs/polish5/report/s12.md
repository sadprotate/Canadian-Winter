## 12.0 Audit (before any change)

**The pickup's numbers** (`data/vehicles/pickup.tres`, read by the one shared controller
`scripts/vehicles/vehicle.gd`): `clearance` 0.30 m, `traction` 0.55, `max_speed` 15 m/s, width 2.0,
length 5.4; the model's front bumper bar is 0.36–0.54 m high, the grille top 0.95 m, the hood 1.15 m.
Every vehicle's `clearance` (m): ATV 0.26, pickup 0.30, pickup with plow 0.34, dump truck 0.42, plow
truck 0.48, tractor (blade) 0.50, grader / tractor (blower) 0.55, rotary blower 0.80.

**The checks** (per physics frame, every vehicle, `_physics_process`):
- *Snow under the wheels* `d_wheels` = the mean depth at the four (six) wheel positions. Drag on the
  speed limit `1 / (1 + 2.5 (d/c)²)` and on acceleration `1 / (1 + d/c)` (c = clearance): the pickup
  did 33 km/h in 15 cm, 15 km/h in 30 cm.
- *Stuck* when `d_wheels > 1.15 c` (pickup: 34.5 cm): speed limit × 0–0.25 (to 0 over the next
  0.5 c), throttle × 0.15 — it doesn't move.
- *Snowbank* `ahead` = the deepest of three probes 0.35 m ahead of the front, across ±0.35 × width:
  with throttle and `ahead > 1.9 c` (pickup: 57 cm) the vehicle **stops dead, at any speed** (speed set
  to 0 the same frame). A plow with its blade down or a blower running caps `ahead` at 1.5 c, so they
  never hit this check.
- *Messages* (polish 2 §4, `blocked_reason` → `set_status`, every 2–2.5 s while blocked):
  "Stuck: N cm of snow under the wheels — this pickup truck only clears about M cm. Dig around the
  wheels and clear a path." (M = 115 c) and "Snowbank ahead: N cm deep — too much to drive through
  (about M cm at most). Clear it or back up." (M = 190 c); on getting in, "The pickup truck is snowed
  in: about N cm of snow around the wheels…" when `d_wheels > 1.15 c`; the HUD line adds "STUCK". No
  keys in them.
- *How the plows push:* blade down (`veh_tool`), each frame one `Snow.blade_push` at the blade —
  face along the travel direction turned by the blade angle (Z / C), half-width = blade width / 2 ×
  the width upgrade, band = the frame's advance + 0.1 m, floor `blade_floor` (1.5 cm: down to the
  pavement), no road rule since §3, the snow put 0.6 m ahead of the face; `player_guard` marks the 2 m
  behind the blade so relaxation can't slump snow back into the lane; the berm (snow within 0.65 m
  ahead) slows it: speed limit × 1 / (1 + (berm / capacity)²) (pickup with plow 2.8 m³, plow truck
  8 m³); snow moved counts in the season stats; bursts of powder at the blade's side.
- Nothing else changes the snow for a vehicle (no tracks); the pickup without a plow never moved snow.

**Measured on this branch before §12** (the probe: the pickup on Maple Street, fixed 60 fps, real
throttle; `before_*.log`): see the before columns in 12.2. In short: drivable up to 30 cm, stuck from
35 cm; any bank of 60 cm or more stopped it dead whatever its speed; a long stretch of 40–50 cm
stopped it after ~12 m; the windrow a 25 or 30 cm storm leaves across the apron (71 / 85 cm) kept it
in the driveway; the opening: stuck (80 cm around the wheels).

## 12.1 The capability (`scripts/vehicles/vehicle.gd`, the plain pickup only)

Everything below applies to `VehicleData.Kind.PICKUP`; every other vehicle runs the same lines with
its own `clearance` exactly as before (the expressions are kept as they were for them).

- **Under the wheels:** the drag uses `pickup_drag_depth` (0.40 m) as its scale instead of the
  clearance — 40 km/h in 15 cm, 30 in 22.5 cm, 22 in 30 cm. Stuck from `pickup_stuck_depth` (0.60 m,
  `Vehicle.stuck_depth()`; the same "snowed in" check on getting in).
- **The shove** (`Vehicle._shove`, each physics frame while moving forward with snow deeper than
  `pickup_shove_floor` 0.30 m at the front probes): the existing `Snow.blade_push`, twice — one arm
  for each half of the truck's width, a fixed V with its point at the middle of the bumper and its arms
  45° back to the front corners (`SHOVE_SPLAY_DEG`), each arm casting its snow 1.5 m along its face
  (`SHOVE_CAST`): ahead of the truck and out to the side, clear of its path. Floor 0.30 m (no clean
  lane, no pavement), no road rule (§3), no angling key. While it shoves, the truck's footprint is
  guarded from slumping snow (`Snow.set_blade_guard`, as `player_guard` does behind a plow blade).
  Snow moved counts in the season's "Snow moved" like the plows'; powder bursts at the bumper's
  corners. A first version cast the snow 0.5 m at 30°: the berm it left right in front of the bumper
  read as deeper snow at the front probe, so the truck bogged on its own berm in 40 cm — casting it
  clear of the path fixed that.
- **How hard the snow pushes back:** x = (front depth − 0.30) / (`pickup_bog_depth` − 0.30), the
  front depth being the existing three probes 0.35 m ahead of the bumper. The truck loses
  `accel × x + pickup_shove_drag × x × v²` per second (accel = what the engine gives at that moment):
  at x = 1 the snow pushes back as hard as the engine pulls; the v² part makes a fast hit cost more,
  so a faster run-up only carries it a little further. Under the bog depth it keeps going, slower the
  deeper (~6 km/h in 60 cm); over it only its speed carries it, and below
  `pickup_shove_min_speed` (1 m/s) it stops ("bogged": `max_fwd` 0, speed 0).
- **The wall:** snow ahead deeper than `pickup_bank_max` (0.95 m, the grille's top) stops it at any
  speed — the old bank check with the pickup's own limit.
- Reversing doesn't shove (the wheel checks only), and neither does a standing truck.

Balance values (`data/balance.tres` and the defaults in `scripts/data/balance_data.gd`, new group
"Pickup truck"):

| value | | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `pickup_drag_depth` | 0.40 m | depth scale of the drag under the wheels |
| `pickup_stuck_depth` | 0.60 m | stuck with more than this under the wheels (was 1.15 × 0.30 = 34.5 cm) |
| `pickup_shove_floor` | 0.30 m | snow up to this passes under the bumper; above it the front shoves, down to it |
| `pickup_bog_depth` | 0.70 m | front snow pushing back as hard as the engine pulls |
| `pickup_shove_drag` | 0.16 | the speed-squared part of the push-back |
| `pickup_shove_min_speed` | 1.0 m/s | below it, in snow over the bog depth, it stops |
| `pickup_bank_max` | 0.95 m | a wall at any speed (was 1.9 × 0.30 = 57 cm, at any speed) |

## 12.7 Fix: a deterministic deep-snow stop (after §13a)

`test_s12_bogging_down_in_deep_snow` was flaky: in §13a's full run its 90 cm case stopped after
1.7 m (it allowed 1.5 m); alone it gave 1.4 and 0.9 m.

**Cause:** where the shoved snow lands. `Snow.blade_push` (the existing op, unchanged) puts each
cell's snow 0–12 cm further out by a draw from the engine's global random generator (`randf()`),
whose state depends on whatever ran before. At 90 cm, under the 95 cm wall, the pickup shoves in
