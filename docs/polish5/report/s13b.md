## 13b.1 Equipment — its own kind of item

- **`scripts/data/equipment.gd`** (`class_name Equipment`, new): the three ids
  (`insulated_coat`, `snowshoes`, `heated_pad` — none is a tool, vehicle, upgrade or utility id; a
  test checks it), prices from balance, `shown(id)` (the coat only in Survival), `owns(id)` (the
  `ItemUpgrades.owns` pattern: picked up, carried, a crate waiting on the store's pad, or on order;
  one of each), `why_not(id)` ("Survival Mode only." / "Already owned."), `collect(id)`, the coat
  (`coat_worn()`, `cold_mult()`, `migrate()`), the snowshoes (`snowshoes_on()`,
  `toggle_snowshoes()`), the pad (`pad_placed()`, `pad_rect()`, `pad_spot_problem()`,
  `put_down_pad()`, `pick_up_pad()`), the Tab/Property lines (`overview_lines(keys)`), and the §16
  hook `Equipment.extra_property` (more world rects that are yours).
- **Catalog**: `Catalog.EQUIPMENT` (name, the balance key of its price, a description with
  `{action}` keys — shown through `Settings.with_keys`); `Catalog.item_name` knows them.
- **Economy**: `item_kind` → `"equipment"`; `price_of`; `can_buy` adds `Equipment.why_not`.
  Bought → an order of kind `"equipment"` like any other (ready during store hours, else at 7:00).
- **Store**: an equipment order is a crate on the pickup pad (`crate:<id>`), like an upgrade.
  **Collecting it** (`ToolItem._open_crate`, Interact — already through `Settings.accept_press`):
  the coat is put on, the snowshoes are on, the pad goes into your arms (`Game.carried`); a pad crate
  stays at the store while your arms are full ("Your arms are full. Put down what you're carrying
  first (G).").
- **State**: `Game.equipment` (id → state: the coat `{}`, the snowshoes `{"on": bool}`, the pad `{}`
  carried or `{"pos": [x, y, z], "yaw": f}` put down) — the world-object registry; saved as the
  optional version-3 key `game.equipment`.
- **The store page**: an "Equipment" section after the hand tools and machines, a card each with
  its icon (`Icons.tool(id)`; drawn for equipment — see "Readings"), the keys read from the input
  map, a stats line (the coat's −40 %, the snowshoes' sink, the pad's size, rate and power).
- **The Tab overview and the Property app** list the equipment ("Coat: insulated coat (worn)",
  "Snowshoes: on (E to take them off)", "Heated pad: down at …").

## 13b.2 The insulated coat — it replaces the jacket and the parka

The old `clothing_1` (Insulated Work Jacket, $150, ×0.8) and `clothing_2` (Arctic Parka & Mitts,
$420, ×0.75) were sold in both modes in the Upgrades app. **Reading taken: the coat replaces them**
(the change that avoids two overlapping coats, and changes least for an old save):
- the coat's fraction (`coat_cold_protection` **0.4**, loss × 0.6) is the two upgrades together
  (0.8 × 0.75), so the one coat is "a better coat" than the jacket and protects exactly as the full
  old line did; price `coat_price` **$480** (the full line was $570);
- `clothing_1` / `clothing_2` are retired (`Catalog.RETIRED_UPGRADE_IDS`: still loaded for old
  saves, orders and crates; `can_buy` → "No longer sold."); the Upgrades app no longer has a "Cold
  protection" section (an empty category shows no heading);
- **old saves** (`Equipment.migrate`, from `Game.deserialize`): either upgrade → the coat (owned,
  worn in Survival), the clothing ids leave `game.upgrades`; protection ≥ what was paid for (both:
  × 0.6 = × 0.6; the jacket alone: × 0.8 → × 0.6); one notification in Survival ("Your jacket and
  parka are now one insulated coat: the same warmth, worn as before."), none in Fun Mode; an old
  jacket or parka crate collected later (`Game.grant_upgrade`) is the coat.
- **Effect**: `Survival.outdoor_loss_per_hour` and the cold-house loss multiply by
  `Equipment.cold_mult()` (was `Game.stat_value(&"cold_protection")`). §14's night multiplier
  multiplies the same exposure, so the coat applies at night too.
- **Fun Mode** (Decision 9): not sold ("Survival Mode only."), not worn, not shown — even when an
  old Fun save owns it.
- **The look**: a long red down parka over the orange jacket (`CharacterModel.set_coat`: quilted
  body to the thighs, front placket, the hood folded down with a fleece collar, puffy sleeves with
  fleece cuffs; small bevels so no corner of the jacket shows through) and the same red sleeves with
  fleece cuffs in first person (`FPArms.set_coat`, the jacket's sleeves hidden).
  `Player.refresh_equipment()` on `Game.inventory_changed`.

Measured (`test_s13b_the_coat`): moderate snow, wind chill → 4.81 → 2.88 %/h outdoors; a house with
no heat −0.92 → −0.55 %/h. Both `2_upgrades` fixtures: the jacket and parka (× 0.60) → one coat, no
clothing upgrade left; Survival: × 0.60 ≤ × 0.60, one notification, the coat on the character;
Fun: owned, not worn or shown, no notification; sleep → autosave → reload keeps it, no second note.

## 13b.3 Snowshoes

- **On/off from the Tab panel**: the overview is a hold-Tab panel with the mouse captured, so the
  toggle is a key while it's up — **hold Tab and press Interact** (E): the line says
  "Snowshoes: on (E to take them off)" (keys from `Settings.action_label`); the press goes through
  `Settings.accept_press("interact")` in `game_root._unhandled_input` (which ignores everything
  while `Game.typing()`), and replaces Interact's world action while the overview is up. Reading
  taken: no new input action (no new default, no conflict, no settings migration).
- **On by default** once picked up (`Equipment.collect`).
- **Standing on the snow**: the body's capsule stays on the ground (walls, house fronts, roofs'
  supports and the map's invisible walls stop you exactly as before — no roofs, no map edge); the
  feet, the model and the camera stand on the snow under you: `Player.shoe_lift` (the depth under the
  feet, eased at 3 m/s), `Player.feet_y()`; the model and the head are raised by it.
- **You sink only part of the way** (`snowshoes_sink` **0.25**): each step on snowshoes treads the
  snow under the shoes down by that fraction (`Snow.wade` with keep 0.75; a cell is trodden once per
  pass — the overlapping wades skip what the last three trod — walking your track again packs it
  further), and you stand on the trodden snow. So the sink *is* the track: one balance value. Reading
  taken: the brief's "sink part way" and "a much shallower trench" are the same compacted snow under
  a snowshoe; one value keeps the feet on the track's floor (with two the shoes would hang inside
  the snow).
- **Wade much faster**: the speed comes from the snow you sink into (`snow_speed_mult(depth ×
  sink)`).
- **Hip rule** from the feet: `can_wade_into` measures the snow ahead from `feet_y()`; the trench's
  max top too.
- **Tools work as usual** (they aim from the raised camera; `ToolController._hand_pos` lifts thrown
  snow from `feet_y()`).
- Off in a vehicle or at a walk-behind machine; indoors there is no snow (lift 0). The snowshoes show
  on the boots (`CharacterModel.set_snowshoes`) while they're on outdoors.

Measured (`test_s13b_snowshoes`, real input, 1 s of walking): bare ground 4.12 / 4.12 m (no change);
0.3 m 2.42 → 3.82; 0.6 m 1.30 → 3.34; **0.85 m 0.63 → 2.96 m/s**. Standing after a few steps on
0.85 m: sunk 0.19 m (23 %; balance 25 %), the model and camera on the snow. The track through 0.8 m:
without 0.34 m left (0.46 m trodden), with **0.60 m left (0.20 m trodden)**, volume conserved. A 1.3 m
bank from 0.8 m snow: a wall without, walked up onto with (feet at 0.6 m); 1.7 m: a wall either way.
Up a 2.4 m drift against the house front: feet up to 1.86 m, the wall still stops you (never
inside). On 3 m of snow at the west map edge: stops at x 0.30 m. A small shovel fills to 50 L on
snowshoes, volume conserved. Tab + E off, and on again.

## 13b.4 The heated pad

- **Carried** in your arms (`Game.carried = "heated_pad"`; the HUD and Tab say "Carrying: Heated
  Snow-Melting Pad (G to drop)"); in first and third person a folded, rolled mat
  (`HeatedPad.carry_mesh`).
- **Put down with G** (`ToolController.drop_tool` → `Equipment.put_down_pad`, already through
  `accept_press("drop")`): 1.9 m in front of you, square to the snow grid (yaw snapped to a quarter
  turn; 1.25 × 2.5 m = exactly 5 × 10 cells). Refused, and kept in your arms with a hint, unless the
  whole footprint is on **your property** (`Equipment.property_rects()`: your lot from its front
  edge — not the sidewalk, boulevard or street — plus `Equipment.extra_property`, the §16 hook), never
  on a road cell ("Not on the street: put the pad down on your own lot."), never on a building, its
  stairs or indoors.
- **Picked up with E** (`HeatedPad`, an `Interactable`: "[E] Pick up the heated pad"), not while
  your arms are full.
- **The model** (`scripts/world/heated_pad.gd`, new): a black rubber mat with the heating cable as
  red ribs and a small control box whose lamp glows orange while it melts (`Heating.running`) and
  goes dark in a power cut. It lies on the ground; snow on it is the snow in its cells.
- **Melts** the snow on it at `pad_melt_cm_h` **30 cm** of depth per game hour (≈ 0.94 m³ an hour
  over its 3.1 m²); loose snow round it slumps onto the melted cells (relaxation), so a load thrown
  on it keeps melting.
- **Power**: `melt_cost_per_m3` for what it melts goes on the next bill; a power cut stops it.
- **Saved with the world objects**: `game.equipment.heated_pad.pos / yaw`; `game_root.refresh_pad()`
  rebuilds its node on load and after G / E.

Measured (`test_s13b_heated_pad`): G on the street, the sidewalk and the neighbour's lot refused
(still carried); on your lot down in front of you; **30.0 cm in a game hour**, snow + melted =
before (residual 0.0000000000 m³, 0.937 m³ melted), $1.41 on the next bill (0.94 m³ × $1.50); a power
cut: nothing melts or is billed, it resumes with the power; the January bill = $85 + $2 heating; a
0.6 m³ throw on it → 0.07 m³ left after 1.5 game hours; saved → loaded where it lay; E picks it up
(not with your arms full).

## 13b.6 Melting: the ledger, the clock, the bill

- **`Snow.melt_rect(r, amount)`** (new): each cell of the rect loses up to `amount` of depth off its
  top (buildings' cells skipped; a float's worth — `MELT_MIN` 1e-6 m, the lazy snowfall's rounding —
  isn't snow); what leaves is measured **after it is stored** (float32 cells) and added to the
  ledger **`Snow.melted`** (m³), so the ledger matches the depth array exactly. Relaxation is queued
  as after any operation. The ledger is per winter (reset with the snow), saved with the snow
  (`snow.melted`, optional). Documented as the third explicit exception at the top of
  `snow_sim.gd` (box completion, the dump truck, melting).
- **`scripts/world/heating.gd`** (`class_name Heating`, new): the sources now (the pad, the heated
  boxes), `advance(step)` — **called from `Clock._systems_advance`**, so it runs on every clock step:
  the frame's step at 1×, every fast-forward substep, every step of a sleep/skip — one code path.
  Each source adds up its game minutes (`Economy.heat_acc`, saved) until they melt `QUANTUM` (2 mm),
  then melts that slice: a pad a few times a second at 1×, a heated box every 12 game minutes — the
  chunks aren't re-meshed every frame.
- **Billing**: `Economy.heat_used` += m³ melted × `melt_cost_per_m3` (**$1.50**; Survival); the next
  hydro bill (`Economy._issue_bill`, the 1st of the month; the first bill of a winter carries the
  last month of the winter before) adds it: `{amount: hydro + heat, heat}`, the notification says
  "(heating $N)". Reading taken: billed per m³ melted (the energy it takes), not per hour switched on —
  a source draws power while it has snow to melt, and a big box with one deep corner isn't billed
  for its whole area. Real cables use ~20 kWh/m³ (≈ $2.40 at 12 ¢); $1.50 keeps the upgrades a
  comfort: the heated driveway costs up to $1.11 an hour while melting, about $70 in a December and
  ~$160 in a stormy January (the test's winter: both boxes $94 / $207).
- **Power cut**: `Survival.power_on` false → every source is off (its minutes dropped, nothing melts
  or is billed); the lamp goes dark.
- **EQUIP_RATE** (§10a): **no entry**. Reading: the pad and the heated boxes melt snow on the
  player's own property (the pad can't go on anyone else's lot; the boxes are the player's own), so
  they never clear a contract job; the snowshoes move the player, they don't clear. The board's
  listing-size cap is unchanged.

**"Every conservation test asserts snow + melted = before, exactly."** The existing conservation
tests run where nothing melts (no pad, no heated box: `Snow.melted` stays 0 — asserted in
`test_s13b_melting_ledger` through 3 game hours of heavy snow) and are unchanged, tolerances
untouched. Every new test where melting runs asserts snow + melted = before:
- `test_s13b_melting_ledger`: with the pad and both heated boxes at work, nine operations on and
  beside them (throws onto the pad and the driveway, a cut, a scoop, a push across the driveway,
  wading across the pad, the grader on Maple Street, the sidewalk crew, relaxation), each followed by
  half an hour of melting (8.08 m³ melted in all). Over every melting stretch: residual
  **0.0000000000 m³** (exact; the ledger holds every m³). Across the whole run (the operations' own
  float32 rounding included): worst 5.4e-7 m³, against the conservation tests' 1e-4.
- `test_s13b_heated_pad` and `test_s13b_heated_driveway_and_path`: residual 0 over their melting.
- Found on the way (pre-existing, not changed): the lazy snowfall keeps each chunk's share in float32
  (`chunk_fall`) against a float64 `fall_total`, so a sync can add a few nm of snow that never fell
  (~3e-7 m³ over a sync of the map after a snowfall). The ledger test snaps `fall_total` to the
  chunks' value first (in the test; snowfall application is in the "leave alone" list).

**1×/2×/10× and skips**: `test_s9_fast_forward_equals_1x` now runs its stretch (a storm, the grader
and crew, bids, contracts) with the heated driveway and path and the pad with a pile on it; the
state compared includes `Snow.melted`, the depth hash, `Economy` (heat_used, heat_acc) and
`Game` (equipment): 2× and 10× end identical to 1×, and 1× melted > 0.3 m³ with a cost on the next
bill. `test_s13b_melting_in_skips_as_in_play`: 2.5 game hours in frame-sized steps and the same
slept through (30-minute steps) melt the same, up to the slice each source is still adding up
(3.032 / 3.106 m³ — one 2 mm slice of the driveway; the pad and driveway depths within 2 mm; $4.55 /
$4.66).

## Readings taken

- **The coat replaces the jacket and the parka** (13b.2): one coat at their combined protection; old
  saves keep (or gain) protection; Fun Mode: no coat anywhere.
- **Equipment icons are drawn** (`Icons._draw_equipment`, through `Icons.tool(id)`): the rendered
