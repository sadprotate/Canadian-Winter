## 4.1 The door line

* **When:** the player opens the front door **from inside** (`player.in_player_house` when the door
  goes from shut to open), during the opening (**stage START**, i.e. before the letter) and with
  **no shovel held** (a `ToolData.Kind.SHOVEL` tool in the slots, carried or on the rack; the one still
  buried outside is in `Game.items` and doesn't count). Checked every frame in
  `game_root._update_door_line()` (a few comparisons).
* **Once:** the first opening from inside in a session decides it (`_door_line_done`); if the stage
  or the shovel says no, it is dropped for good. Nothing is saved (derived from the stage and the
  tools held — no `Game` flag was needed).
* **How it shows:** in the HUD speech box through the same line player as Gerry's (`say_gerry`), as
  speaker "You", timed by its length (`line_seconds`: 4.9 s), Interact (looking at nothing) moves it
  on, no modal, the player never stopped, and no voice (the line is marked `"thought"`; Gerry's
  talk sound is skipped for it).
* **Never two lines at once:** the morning subtitle ("December 1. The storm is over…", at 1.5 s for
  5 s after the game starts on Dec 1 before 8:00) comes first — the door line waits while it is due or
  showing (`_morning_line_due`, `hud.subtitle_box.visible`), or while a modal is open or someone is
  talking. The letter (`open_mailbox`) and any later subtitle (Gerry's "Hey, help!" cry, the radio)
  end a door line still showing. In play the mailbox is 15+ s of wading away, so they never meet.
  Tested: the door opened in the first second of the game, the line comes 368 frames later (right
  after the subtitle), and the speech box and the subtitle box are never up together.

## 4.2 Gerry's two ways to move snow

After the price is agreed (the modal dialogue closes, the contract is created, stage
NEIGHBOR_TALKED — unchanged), he goes on **on the HUD** (`HelpText.snow_tips()`):

1. "Now, two ways to move snow."
2. "Hold [Left Mouse] and sweep through the snow to fill your shovel, then let go to throw it. That gets it up over the banks."
3. "Or hold [Right Mouse] and walk to push it ahead of you. Quick on a long flat walk, but it piles up in front of you."
4. "Clear my walk inside the blue line, from the sidewalk up to my door."

The keys are `Settings.action_label("primary" / "secondary")` when the lines are built (each time he
says them). Matches the game: the sweep-fill / release-to-throw scheme with the throw lifted over
trench walls; the blade push building a mound that slows you (`push_strength`); the quest outline is
light blue and covers his walk from the sidewalk plus his steps (pass 4).

* Delivery as his pass-3 lines: each timed by its length (4.0 / 9.3 / 8.9 / 6.1 s), Interact moves on,
  the player walks throughout (tested: 3.5 m during the first line, no modal, not frozen).
* **Talking to him again during the job repeats the tip** on the HUD. This replaces the old re-talk,
  which was a *modal* one-liner ("Just the path from my steps down to the mailbox — it's marked out
  for you. You're doing great!"): the brief wants the tip, delivered without freezing the player; its
  content is the 4th tip line.
* **Rebound keys:** tested with scoop on J and push on the middle button: "Hold [J] …", "Or hold
  [Middle Mouse] …".
* The tips end when the trail is done (stage TRAIL_DONE; they're about the job). Without this,
  Interact at the meeting by the mailbox would move the tips on instead of starting Gerry's
  invitation — the pass-3 sequence (meet, invite, follow him in) is otherwise unchanged and its tests
  pass as they are.

## 4.4 The photo

### Diagnosis (real renderer: Xvfb, GL Compatibility, llvmpipe; Mesa 25.2.8)

What the picture on the wall showed, and why — each candidate checked:

| Candidate | Result |
|---|---|
| **Capture timing — rendering before the scene is ready** | **The cause of what the playtester saw.** Started as a player starts it (menu → PLAY → New Game, a scene change), the print showed **the truck's underside and front bumper from below, against the sky** — no character, no shovel, no truck side (`before_wall_from_menu*.jpg`, `before_photo_texture_from_menu.jpg`; reproduced on the baseline worktree and on this branch before the fix). That is the photo scene seen from its origin (0, 0, 0) looking down −Z, i.e. with the camera's transform not applied. The viewport was created with `UPDATE_ONCE` and the scene built in the same (deferred) call; its nodes get their places after entering the tree, and those transforms reach the renderer only when the engine flushes its transform notifications. Built during the scene change, the one frame was drawn before that flush. Loading the game scene directly (`tests/shot.tscn`, the tests, the pass-4 screenshots) happens to flush first, which is why pass 4 never saw it. Not slow resources: a capture after the first draw and one 30 frames later are identical (mean abs. difference 0.0000). |
| The placeholder path outside headless | Not it: `DisplayServer` is "X11", the capture runs (`captured = true`, 1338–2265 ms) in both paths. |
| A texture freed with its viewport | Not it: `get_image()` copies; the texture outlives the freed viewport (the print stays after the viewport is gone; the large view shows the same texture minutes later). |
| Material / colour space / exposure under GL Compatibility | **Two faults.** (1) **Mirror-reversed on the wall** in every path: the player's house transform is mirrored (determinant −1), and the UV flip that should undo it was decided in `_ready`, which runs inside `add_child` *before* `make()` sets the transform — so it never flipped (`uv1_scale` (1, 1, 1) at runtime). The truck, the character and the shovel's side were all reversed (`before_wall_noon.jpg` vs `before_photo_texture.jpg`). (2) **A milky veil:** the glass (alpha 0.07, specular 0.9, roughness 0.04) and the print's own sheen (default specular 0.5) reflected the bright sky over the print. Measured on the print at 1.5 m at noon: blacks 0.18 → 0.30, colour (mean saturation) 0.132 → 0.112 (−15 %). Colour space is right (sRGB 8-bit image as sRGB albedo); the rest of the difference from the texture (saturation 0.170) is the room's light and the game's tonemapper — an *unshaded* print measures the same 0.132. |

### Fixes (`scripts/world/framed_photo.gd`)

* **Capture:** the viewport is built with drawing off (`UPDATE_DISABLED`), one process frame goes by
  (the transforms are flushed), then it draws once (`UPDATE_ONCE`) and the image is read. Menu →
  New Game now shows the photo (`after_wall_from_menu*.jpg`). Cost: one more main frame before the
  capture (on this VM's software GL the whole capture took 2.0 → 3.9 s by `shot.tscn` and 2.3 → 2.5 s
  from the menu, with other jobs running; on a GPU, one frame), still behind the load fade.
* **Mirror:** `_orient_print()` decides the flip after `make()` sets the transform.
* **Veil:** a matte print (`metallic_specular` 0) and a fainter hint of glass (alpha 0.04, specular
  0.3): blacks 0.214, saturation 0.128 (glass removed entirely: 0.178 / 0.132). It still darkens with
  the room at night (`after_wall_night.jpg`).

### The hint

* The shovel **stands where it lies buried**: its blade (the tool's `tip()`) is placed at the buried
  shovel's offset from the truck, in the truck's frame, computed from the game's own start spots
  (`Game.truck_start()` — new, the formula moved out of `new_game` unchanged — and
  `Game.shovel_spot()`): (−2.35, −0.20) m = the **driver's side** (the side away from the house), at the
  cab, the middle of the truck just towards the front — the same side and end as the oblong pile
  (centre (−2.39, −0.35)). Planted upright (6° lean), its scoop turned towards the camera, its lowest
  point 5 cm in the snow, all from the mesh itself (`ToolModels.mesh_for("small_shovel")`,
  `ToolModels.tip()`, the mesh's AABB), so §6's new small shovel will stand the same way.
* Pass 4 framed the passenger side (the shovel leaned on that door); the camera, the character, the
  sun and the trees are now mirrored to the shovel's side (they follow the sign of its offset). The
  view is more side-on (camera at (−7.2, 1.3, −1.6) in the truck frame, fov 38°), so the shovel is seen
  against the cab where it really is, not in front of the rear wheel as a front three-quarter view put
  it. Kept: the character facing the camera beside the truck (by the front wheel), a sunny snowy day,
  the warm tint, vignette and white border, no text (`after_photo_texture.jpg`).
* **Readable from 1.5 m at 1080p? No.** The print is 190 × 127 px on screen; the shovel is ~45 px tall,
  its scoop ~15 px wide and its shaft under a pixel — an orange spot (`after_wall_noon.jpg`). So:
  **"[E] Look at the photo"** (an `Interactable` lying flat over the frame, within 3.5 cm of the wall):
  the photo large on screen (1200 × 800 in a mat and frame, the room dimmed), no text; Interact or Esc
  puts it down (`after_photo_view.jpg`). It is a modal like the TV (the player stands still while
  looking). Key from the input map like every prompt.
* Isolation and the headless placeholder are as in pass 4 (`test_photo_scene_is_isolated`,
  `test_photo_on_the_wall_beside_the_door` pass unchanged; the scene only *reads* `Game` and
  `Catalog`).

## Readings taken

* "The opening" for the door line = stage START (before the letter). The pass-3/4 *opening* fixtures
  are at START, so their first opening from inside shows it — that is the opening.
* "Owning a shovel" = a shovel (Kind SHOVEL) in hand, in a slot or on the rack.
* Once per session, derived, no flag: a game saved at stage START (sleeping before ever reading the
  letter) and reloaded shows it again at its first opening. Judged acceptable rather than add saved
  state (the brief prefers derived state).
* Gerry's re-talk during the job became the tips on the HUD (was a modal line) — required by "talking
  to him again during the job repeats the tip" with his pass-3 delivery.
* The tips stop at TRAIL_DONE (see 4.2).
* After the tutorial, talking to him repeats only the keys line until they're found.
* "Within a couple of metres" → under 2.5 m (measured 2.16 m).
* "Stands where it lies buried" → planted upright at the buried spot, the view moved to that side.
* The shovel isn't recognisable on the wall at 1.5 m, so the large view was added (as the brief asks
  in that case). It is a modal (the player stops while looking), like the TV.
* The veil fix (matte print, fainter glass) is part of "material … under GL Compatibility"; the glass
  stays as a hint.
