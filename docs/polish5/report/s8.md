## 8.4 What changed

**One card for the TV and the computer** (`scripts/autoload/weather.gd`):
- `forecast_day(day, issued)` builds a card; `forecast(days)` returns them from today (as before).
  Hours already gone *and the hour now* are what is happening (was: hours already gone); the rest is
  the forecast, as before. New fields: `tier` ("", "light", "storm", "major"), `from_h` / `to_h` (when
  the snow on that day starts and ends, game hours). A system is on a card if it has fallen on that
  day or the forecast puts snow in one of its remaining hours (the same hour mapping `fc_hour` uses,
  so the cards and the hourly forecast can't disagree); the card names the strongest. The icon
  follows the strength named.
- `fc_tier(i, issued, radio)` — a forecast event's own tier; a storm still under-called reads as a
  light snowfall; "" while not shown. `fc_span(i, issued, radio)` — its forecast start and end.
- `card_text(entry)` → `{date, title, amount, when, temps}`: "Snowstorm", "15–25 cm" ("Under 1 cm"
  for a trace), "from about 2 p.m." / "from about 2 p.m. to 10 p.m." / "until about 10 a.m." / "all
  day" (a midnight that would read as the wrong end of the day is "overnight" or "until just after
  midnight"), "High −5° · Low −12°". `card_line(entry)` = "Snowstorm · 15–25 cm · from about 2 p.m.".
- The strength in words: `TIER_NAMES` / `tier_name(t)` ("Light snow", "Snowstorm", "Major
  snowstorm"), `cond_tier(c)`, used by the TV, the computer and the radio.
- **The TV** (`Panels.tv`): each card shows the date, the icon, the strength (or Clear / Cloudy), the
  range, when, the high and low. One line more per card in the same screen: the card's icon 88 → 72
  px, its spacing 6 → 2 px, its top/bottom padding 10.8 → 8 px. The screen, bezel, rows, the "Right
  now" block, the alert and grader lines and "Forecast updated" are as before. `Panels.tv(parent,
  on_close, jingle := true)`.
- **The computer's Weather app**: the TV's cards — at least its three days (`maxi(3,
  forecast_days())`; WeatherWise Pro and Radar+ still add days, the wind, and the radar's systems
  hour by hour), "Next N days · forecast updated 9 a.m.". `forecast_text(entry, detail)` is the card
  line, the high and low, then the apps' detail.

**Decision 7** (`_fc_view`): a storm that arrives stronger than forecast is called at its real
strength from 7:00 a.m. on the day it starts (`CALL_HOUR`, `called_by(st, issued)`), by every
forecast (TV, computer, radio); within the radio's 3-hour horizon it already was (and still is, TV
too, as in pass 4). An unforecast major event is never shown by the TV view before it starts; the
radio has it from 3 h ahead (`radio_surprise_horizon_h` 3).

**The radio** (`radio_bulletin`): "Right now …" and the three hourly phrases as in pass 4, then from
start to end, for the system falling now and the next one due in its window (at most two):
"Snowstorm ending around 6 p.m., another 20 to 26 cm." (falling), "Major snowstorm ending around
1 a.m. tomorrow, 90 to 110 cm in all." (due), "Light snow ending around 2 p.m." (under 1 cm to come),
or "Snow has ended." for 3 game hours after the last snow ended. The amount is the radio's forecast
of the rest of the system (the rest of this hour as it falls, then hour by hour to its end), its
range the forecast error at that lead; the end is the forecast end (exact once it has started).
`radio_spells(issued)`, `radio_spell(issued)`, `radio_spell_text()`, `RADIO_WINDOW_H` (3). The house
radio reads `game_root.radio_text()` (the bulletin + the grader line, as before); the truck's radio
the same bulletin.

**The hour** (`game_root._on_hour_displays`, on `Clock.hour_changed`): a TV left on is rebuilt with
the new issue (no jingle); a radio bulletin still on screen is read again for the new hour.

**Keys**: nothing new is shown or read; no key handling added outside `game_root`.

## Readings taken

- **"Forecast events"** = the schedule's non-surprise events; the two kinds of surprise follow
  Decision 7 and are tested on their own.
- **"Three days out" / "the day before"** = the event's (card's) day − the issuing day, at every issue
  hour of the issuing day (worst case, up to 96 h for three days out). The start-time targets are
  applied to the time shown (rounded to the hour), the stricter reading, and to the model.
- **"The range shown contains the actual day total"** — measured on days whose snow comes only from
  forecast events, with the integer range as displayed ("Up to 3 cm" = 0–3); all snowy days are
  reported too.
- **The radio's targets** — on the calls where the truth or the radio has snow (all calls are 99.2 %
  right anyway); "the rate within ±25 %" = at least 95 % of the snowy hours (≥ 0.2 cm/h) within ±25 %.
- **The tier on a card** is the strongest system snowing on that day; a system that truly starts or
  ends within an hour of the day may be named (the "about" of the shown time; 12 cards in 22,952,
  counted in the audit).
- **Sync and Decision 7.** "If the radio calls snow in its window, the TV's today card shows snow then"
  holds in every hour except the one difference Decision 7 makes: the radio has an unforecast major
  event up to 3 h before the TV, which never shows it before it starts. Those hours (37 in 5 seeds ×
  winters 1–3) are counted apart; in them the TV still shows whatever is falling.
- **"Before it starts"** stays pass 4's: before its start time. In the partial hour it starts in, the
  TV's today card shows the snow falling (the lead-in, "Light snow") and the next hour's issue names
  it — with the onset alert. (Revealing it at the top of that hour would have changed the pass-4
  surprise test.)
- **7:00 a.m.** applies to every forecast (TV, computer, radio), not only the today card, so the
  three never disagree about a storm's strength.
