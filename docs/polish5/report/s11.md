## 11.0 Diagnosis — the city before §11

**How the grader is scheduled.** `Municipal.start_winter()` seeds its stream with `weather_seed × 3 +
year` and draws the first pass's day (`randi_range(2, 3)`: Dec 3 or 4) and a time of day
(`randf_range(4, 8)` h, e.g. 7.60 h = 7:35 for seed 2024); each pass then adds 3 or 4 days (a 50/50 draw), so
every pass of a winter is at that same minute. The pass starts in `Municipal.on_hour`, at the first
top of the hour at or after `next_pass` — so the seed-2024 grader "expected Dec 4 07:35" really
comes at 8:00. With the world loaded (and not skipping) `GraderNPC` drives the route at 6.5 m/s on
the clock's substeps (§9); in a skip, or without a world, `run_batch` applies the same lane op to
every lane at once. The TV ("City grader next expected: Dec 4 07:35"), the radio ("City grader
expected Dec 4 07:35.") and the computer ("Next pass: …") showed the stored `next_pass`. **The
stale date** (§8's note): when the clock is *set* (`Clock.reset`, as tests and shots do) nothing
moves `next_pass`, so a shot on Jan 18 showed "Dec 4"; and at the next hour the grader would start a
catch-up pass and add only 3–4 days — still in the past — so it passed again and again (every hour
in a skip) until it caught up.

**What its pass leaves on a street** (`Snow.grader_lane`, unchanged). Two lanes per section, 1.95 m
either side of the centre line, each a 3.4 m blade band: the band is cut to a ragged residual of
3 cm ± 90 % (noise), with skipped patches left at 55 % of their depth where the noise is above
0.45 and the outer 0.3 m of the blade at least 45 % — "the little accumulation". Between the two
bands 0.5 m is never touched — "the bank in the middle of the street" — and 0.35 m at each curb.
The snow goes into a windrow per 25 cm slice, 1.9–3.4 m from the lane centre on its push side
(toward its own curb), weights 12 / 20 / 22 / 20 / 14 / 8 / 4 %: 12 % on the street's last cell
before the curb, the rest across the 1.5 m boulevard, heaviest in its first metre from the curb, and
across driveway aprons the same way (the apron's boulevard part). At the 6 m ceiling it spills ring
by ring, roads allowed.

**What happens to the sidewalks.** Nothing clears them. Every lot has a 28 m sidewalk box (24 boxes,
all on Maple Street and Birch Avenue; Cedar Lane's sidewalks aren't boxes; a box includes the
sidewalk's crossing of a driveway, which the driveway box shares). The windrow is loose snow, and
relaxation slumps it off the boulevard onto the sidewalk and the front lawns (only the curb is a
wall). Measured (`depth_report`, Birch Avenue, seed 2024): the sidewalks hold as much as an untouched
yard all winter (1.34 m on Dec 15 against the yard's 1.28 m; 3.70 m on Mar 31) and the boulevards
3.1 → 6.0 m.

**Where the grader puts its snow** — on the boulevard's street side. So the sidewalk crew's mound
goes on the same strip, on its sidewalk side (below), beside the windrow rather than on top of it.

## 11.1 Weekdays (`Clock`)

The game had a placeholder (`(day + year × 2) % 7`: winter 1 starting on a Wednesday) and no
years. Now `Clock.real_date(day, year)` maps a winter day to its real date — winter N runs from
December of 2025 + N to March of 2026 + N (`FIRST_WINTER_YEAR` 2026) — and
`Clock.weekday_index(day, year)` (0 = Monday) / `Clock.weekday(day, year)` ("Tue", `WEEKDAYS`)
read the real calendar (days from 1970-01-01). Dec 1 of winters 1–7: Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun, Mon,
Wed. `Clock.weekday` is the one function every display already read (the HUD clock's date line, the
computer's clock and lock screen, due times through `Contracts.when_text`, the map's "due Fri"
tag); the TV's and the computer's forecast cards now read it too (`Weather.card_text`'s date:
"Fri Dec 4"). No test hard-coded a weekday (the §10a/§10b tests already used `Clock.weekday`).

**The leap day.** The game's February has 28 days (`MONTH_START`) and winter 2 runs into 2028.
Reading taken (changes the least): the game's calendar and day count are untouched and every date
keeps its *real* weekday, so the leap day is simply not played — in winter 2 Feb 28, 2028 is a
Monday and the game's next day, March 1, a Wednesday (the same in winter 6, 2032). That week has
one day fewer: its Monday pass (Feb 28) is followed by Friday's (Mar 3) three game days later.

## 11.2 The schedule (`Municipal`)

- **Days:** Mondays and Fridays (`CREW_DAYS` [0, 4]); `is_crew_day(day)`.
- **Time:** the grader's own — the same two draws from its stream as before (the old first day is
  drawn and no longer used, the time of day is kept), and the pass starts at the top of the hour
  after it, as `on_hour` always started it (`pass_minute()`: seed 2024 draws 7.60 h → 8 a.m., seed 7
  7.67 → 8 a.m., seed 99 5.24 → 6 a.m.). Derived from the seed and the winter, so it isn't saved.
- **The next pass:** `next_crew_time(after)` (pure: the first Monday / Friday at that hour strictly
  after `after`); `next_pass` is set from it at the start of a winter and after each pass (the
  3-or-4-day draw is gone). A pass more than an hour overdue when the hour comes means the clock was
  set past it: `on_hour` puts `next_pass` back on the schedule instead of running a catch-up pass at
  an odd hour. Tests and F1 that set `next_pass = Clock.minutes` to force a pass still work.
- **Old saves:** a saved `next_pass` that isn't a crew start (every save before §11) goes on the
  schedule from the time it was saved at; a new save keeps its own. No new save key (version 3).
- **Passes per winter:** 34 (winter 1, every seed tried), as many as the old 3–4 days gave on
  average. The first pass of winter 1 is always Friday, Dec 4 (it was Dec 3 or 4). A winter starting
  on a Monday or Friday (winters 3 and 6) has its first pass on Dec 1 if the crews' hour is after
  the 7:30 a.m. start, else on the next crew day.

## 11.3 The sidewalk crew

**Route** (`Municipal.crew_runs()`, `crew_route()`): every sidewalk box, in the order the grader
passes it — for each lane of every road section in the grader's order, the sidewalk on the side
that lane pushes its snow to, driven the way the lane goes (the section a player's street contract
takes off the grader's route still gets its sidewalks). 24 boxes: Maple west (south side eastbound,
north side westbound), Maple east, Birch west, Birch east.

**The operation** (`Snow.sidewalk_clear(rect, out)`, new, the only snow op this section adds):
column by column across the sidewalk, every cell to 0 (and held, like a completed box, so the
windrow and the mound can't slump back onto it), and the column's snow straight out toward the
street onto the strip between the sidewalk and the street — the cells up to the curb (the
boulevard, or a driveway's apron) — as a mound on the strip's sidewalk side: `CREW_MOUND` 30 / 30 /
20 / 12 / 6 / 2 % from the sidewalk out, each cell at most up to the ceiling. The grader's windrow
lies on the street side (4 / 8 / 14 / 20 / 22 / 20 % from the sidewalk out), so the two are side by
side: in the test, over 21 boxes, the crew's snow is centred 0.47 m out from the sidewalk (peak in
the first two rows), the windrow's 0.93 m (peak in row 4). What a column's strip has no room for
goes into any room left on the strip, then onto the street's edge, outward from the curb (Decision
12). What each cell receives is read back from the float32 map, so what's placed is counted exactly.
Then the box completes (`Municipal.clear_sidewalk`): with nothing left in it, the 90 % snap removes
nothing; quietly (`Boxes.quiet`: no completion flash or chime — the player didn't do it).

**Skips:** a box with an open player job (`Contracts.contract_for_box(id)`: any live contract,
e.g. a city sidewalk contract) is left alone (`Municipal.crew_skips`); the grader runs its route
as before.

**Batch** (sleep, F1 jumps, no world): `Municipal.run_batch` — the grader's lanes as before, then
`clear_sidewalk` for every box the crew hasn't done, so the grader's snow never lands on a cleared
sidewalk. **In the world:** `SidewalkCrew` (`scripts/vehicles/sidewalk_crew.gd`, new): the ATV
model from `VehicleModels` in the city's orange, its 1.5 m blade angled 22° to cast toward the
street (raised over a skipped box), two beacons on a mast, the small engine loop, a collision box.
It starts with the grader (`Municipal.pass_started`), drives each box at 5.5 m/s, never less than
`LAG` 15 m behind the grader on the same lane (it waits at a box's start until the grader is far
enough ahead; free on a lane the grader is done with or doesn't drive), stops and honks for the
player in its way like the grader (10 s), clears the cells its blade has passed every half metre
(`Snow.sidewalk_clear` on whole columns) and completes each box at its end. It is "busy" on the box
it's plowing (`Boxes.busy`): measured but not completed by the 90 % snap mid-run — found on the
way: before this, the round-robin measurement completed a box once the plow had moved 90 % of it
and the snap removed the last 10 % (−28.8 m³ in a real-time pass). The pass is over
(`pass_finished`) when the grader's lanes and the crew's boxes are all done; sleep finishes the
rest as a batch (`game_root.finish_grader_now`). Teleports between runs, like the grader between
lanes.

**On the clock** (§9): its step runs in the same per-step tick as the grader's — `_physics_process`
at 1× (after the grader's: added after it), `game_root.sim_tick` for each extra substep (after
`grader.sim_step`). `game_root.ff_hold()` returns `"crew"` while it's within 40 m of the player
("Held at 1× · sidewalk crew passing"); `ff_cap()` caps 10× at 2× while it's out on its own
("sidewalk crew out"; while the grader is out its cap already applies).
