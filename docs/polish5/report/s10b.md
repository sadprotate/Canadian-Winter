## 10b.1 No flags — the jobs' outlines

**Removed:** the orange stakes with a flag at each zone corner and the orange line on the snow
(`ZoneMarkers` built them for every incomplete zone of an active / needs-service contract that
wasn't Gerry's). **Kept:** the completion flash and chime (`_on_box_completed`, unchanged).
`ZoneMarkers._stakes` stays as an always-empty dictionary (the pass-3/4 "no flags on Gerry's trail"
checks read it); the stake mesh is gone.

**Outlines** (`ZoneMarkers`, rewritten around BoxOutline's quest mode — the mode Gerry's trail uses):
- `ZoneMarkers.is_job(c)`: status `active` or `needs_service`, not the tutorial (the same contracts the
  stakes were for). Every incomplete box of such a job gets one `BoxOutline` child in quest mode
  (`JobOutline_<box id>`, `quest_fn` = the box), drawn while the box is incomplete; it goes when the
  box completes (at once, by `box_completed`) or the job ends.
- **Colour:** `BoxOutline.JOB_COLOR` (0.07, 0.42, 0.17) — a dark money green — with opacity 0.92 by
  day, 0.85 at night (`JOB_ALPHA`), dimmed at night to 0.85 of itself (`JOB_NIGHT_KEEP`; the blue
  keeps 0.78 in quest mode, 0.6 in work mode). It is dark and doesn't glow, so it needs less dimming
  than the light blue to stay quiet; on the blue night snow it reads clearly green
  (`after_outline_night.jpg`). Gerry's trail (`quest_outline`) and every other box stay
  `BoxOutline.COLOR` (blue).
- **While working:** the work outline (`box_outline`) asks `job_fn` (= `ZoneMarkers.is_job_box`) when
  it takes a box and at each re-drape: a job's box is drawn in the same green. The job's persistent
  outline gives way to it (`BoxOutline.defer_to`: its alpha follows 1 − the work outline's), so the
  box is never drawn twice; when the work outline fades a few seconds after the work stops, the
  persistent one comes back. Gerry's trail keeps the old rule (the work outline skips it).
- **Streets and parking lots** are drawn within `BoxOutline.DRAW_RADIUS` 30 m of the player, as before
  (the same `_big` path; a box with no edge within 30 m now draws nothing instead of logging "No
  vertices were added").
- **Only nearby boxes, at a low rate:** scanned every `SCAN_S` 0.25 s; a box within `LAY_RADIUS` 50 m
  of the player (to its nearest edge) is laid, nearest first, `LAY_PER_SCAN` 1 per scan, and dropped
  beyond `DROP_RADIUS` 60 m. Re-draped on the snow every `REDRAPE_NEAR_S` 0.35 s within `NEAR_DIST`
  8 m of the player (the work outline's rate; a street or parking lot `REDRAPE_NEAR_BIG_S` 1.2 s, as
  the work outline re-drapes one); farther away at most every `REDRAPE_FAR_S` 1.5 s and only once the
  box's average depth changed by `REDRAPE_DEPTH` 1 cm (or the player moved 3 m along a street, or
  6 s went by — `BoxOutline.redrape_depth`, `REDRAPE_MAX_S`, `REDRAPE_MOVE`). Re-drapes are staggered
  (golden-ratio phases, no randomness).
- **Cost, found and fixed on the way** (first version: p95 +2 ms among two dozen job boxes, a 19 ms
  frame when a parking lot was laid):
  - `Boxes.outline_segments` built a dictionary of every cell of the box (the school lot: 27,800
    cells, 15.3 ms). It now walks each rect's border cells only — every boundary edge lies on a rect's
    border — and keeps the result per box (validated against the box's rects). Probe
    (`probe/segs.gd`, all 79 boxes incl. Gerry's trail): **identical edge sets**, 91.9 → 17.5 ms for
    all (school lot 15.3 → 0.86 ms), 0.26 ms cached.
  - `BoxOutline._corner_h` read `Snow.surface_height` at the four cell centres around each corner
    (4 bilinear reads each = 16 cell reads); it now reads the four cells directly through the new
    `Snow.cell_surface(cx, cz)` — exactly the values `surface_height` returns at a cell centre (its
    weights are 0 there), a quarter of the reads. The line sits where it did.

**Perf** (`perf.tscn -- --only=jobs`, new scenario: 20 jobs, 24 boxes — driveways and paths on eight

## 10b.4 The board

**In place** (`ComputerUI`): the board remembers its cards (`_board_cards`: lid → card, its second
line, its status, its controls' signature `_card_form`) and its header + listing order
(`_board_head`). A live refresh (a bid, a rival's bid, the 2 s refresh) with the same header and
listings updates each card's second line (bids, close, snow) and status text / colour in place;
a card whose controls change (your first bid / withdrawn: Place bid ↔ Change bid + Withdraw; closed;
locked) is rebuilt alone in its place. New or removed listings, a reputation change, opening the
app: the whole board as before.

the Change bid button does), besides the rivals' ~25 bids; three alternating pairs, avg / p95 / max ms:
