## 5.1 Addresses — one source: the layout

**The layout had addresses** (`Layout._make_lot`: `number`, `street`, `address`), unchanged since
the first build, so they are kept exactly: **24 and 28 Maple Street are lots 9 and 10** (the
playtest's lots; adjacent built lots on the south side of Maple Street east of Cedar Lane, x
104–160, nothing between them; neither the player's, 16 Maple Street = lot 7, nor Gerry's, 15 Maple
Street = lot 1). §16 can identify them. The scheme as the layout has it: odd numbers on one side,
even on the other, rising by 4 per 28 m lot — Maple Street and Birch Avenue: odd on the north side
(11–31), even on the south side (12–32), rising west to east. In both cases the odd side is the
left side going the way the numbers rise.

**The school, the store and the church had none.** They take the street their parking lots open
onto (their aprons are on Cedar Lane; none of them touches Maple or Birch), in the same scheme:
rising north to south from the lane's north end (as Maple and Birch rise from x = 0), odd numbers on
the left going up (the east side), 4 per 28 m of frontage:

| Building | Side of Cedar Lane | Property along the lane | Address |
|---|---|---|---|
| Northern Supply Co. | east | z 6–42 | **11 Cedar Lane** |
| Maple Ridge School | west | z 5–42 | **12 Cedar Lane** |
| St. Andrew's Church | west | z 192–219 | **40 Cedar Lane** |

The snow dump has no building and keeps its name only. Each parking lot is named by its building and
address. In `Layout`: `buildings[i]` gain `number`, `street`, `address`, `place` ("Maple Ridge
School, 12 Cedar Lane") and `number_at`; `big_areas[i]` gain `building`, `address`, `place`;
`place_name(target)` is the one name for a contract target / box owner ("lot:9" → "24 Maple
Street", "area:store_lot" → "Northern Supply Co., 11 Cedar Lane", "road:maple_w" → "Maple Street
(west)"); `current_place(text)` maps the old parking-lot names in saved text; `building_by_id(id)`.
The scheme is written at the top of `layout.gd`.

## 5.2 In the world

**Houses — the plate** (`AddressSigns.house_plate`, built by `HouseBuilder.build` as the house
root's child `HouseNumber`): a 0.56 × 0.32 m dark plate with a painted cream border and 0.21 m cream
digits, on the front wall beside the door, centred under the existing wall lantern (the lantern at
x −0.55 / y 2.0 of the house frame, the plate at y 1.6, clear of the door casing), facing the
street, never mirrored (the mirrored lots, 32 Maple Street and 12 Birch Avenue, read the right way:
`after_houses_*.jpg`, bottom left). From the foot of the walk (the sidewalk, eye 1.6 m above the
snow there) it is in direct view of every house and its digits are ≥ 10.7 px tall on a 1080-line
screen at the default 75° field of view (tested for all 22).

**At night:** no light was added. The porch lantern just above the plate is its light: the plate's
material (`shaders/address_plate.gdshader`, `Mats.address_paint`) adds the lantern's warm light in
proportion to the paint's own colour, scaled by `night_factor` and fading with the distance from the
lantern — so the cream digits and border read and the dark plate stays dark; nothing is self-lit
(`after_24_maple_night.jpg`, `after_houses_night.jpg`). By day it is ordinary matte paint.

**The mailbox:** it fits — 0.085 m cream digits on the mailbox's door (the 0.26 m end facing the
street), a child `MailboxNumber` of the mailbox node; the mailboxes and lamps are otherwise unchanged
(same nodes, places, counts: the pass-3/4 mailbox and lamp tests pass as they are).

**Other buildings** (`AddressSigns.building_number`, a `Label3D` in the lettering of their name
signs, unshaded like them, digits 1.0 m tall — about 5× a house's): none of the three has a modelled
door, so "the main entrance" is read as the front, where its name is, and the number goes where the
street sees it: the **school's** east end wall by its front corner (and the flagpole), facing the
lane across its lot; the **store's** front beside its name board; the **church's** east wall at the
tower end, facing its lot and the lane (its front, with "ST. ANDREW'S", faces the map's edge, out of
sight from any street). From the entrance of each lot on Cedar Lane each number is in direct view,
12–48° off its facing, 15–24 px tall at 1080p.

**Rendering and cost:** the game's font (Open Sans SemiBold, `ThemeDB.fallback_font` — what the UI
uses): the plates and mailboxes as meshes (`MeshKit.text`: the glyph outlines of a `TextMesh`, front
faces only, into the kit), the buildings through `Label3D`. Every sign is its own node with a
visibility range — plates 35 m (their digits are ~4 px there), mailboxes 20 m, buildings 90 m — no
shadows; meshes are shared per number (`plate:24`, `mailbox:24`), so a world build makes 12 + 12
small meshes once (~30 ms, ~1 ms on every later build).

## Readings taken

- Cedar Lane's numbers (11, 12, 40): rising north to south, odd on the east side (the left going up,
  as on the other streets), 4 per 28 m of frontage — so the church, 170 m down the lane, is 40 rather
  than the next number.
- "By the main entrance" for buildings with no modelled door: on the front (where the name is) or,
  where that front doesn't face the street, on the wall beside it that does (school, church); one
  number per building; no doors added.
- "Readable": digits ≥ 10 px tall on a 1080-line screen at the default field of view; houses from the
  foot of the walk, buildings from their lot's entrance on the street.
- "At night": the existing porch lantern is the small porch light; the plate takes its light in its
  material (no real light, no glow of its own).
- A parking lot's place is "‹building›, ‹address›"; the client's name on its listings stays the
  building's.
- "The same address everywhere" includes saved games: names are re-derived from the layout on load
  (contracts, listings, bids, dynamic boxes) or mapped (history, transactions) — no save key, no save
  version change.
- The map legend's "(no snow allowed!)" was removed while reworking the map (stale since §3).
- The map's parking-lot labels still repeat their building's name, and the snow dump is unlabelled on
  the map (as before; it is in the legend).
