## 10.0 Audit — how it worked before §10

**Listings.** `Contracts._generate_listings(day, initial)` ran at winter start (6 listings, day 0)
and every day at 6:00 (`on_hour`), filling the board up to 5, from the board's own stream
`_rng_for(day * 17 + 5)`. Each try: roll < 0.12 and the city unlocked → a street section; < 0.24 and
"big jobs" unlocked → a parking lot; otherwise a random lot (not vacant, the player's or Gerry's)
with one of five zone sets (driveway, path, driveway + path, path + sidewalk, sidewalk), recurring
45 % of the time after day 0. Price `_price(volume)` (base fee + 4.2 × m³^0.75, recurring × 0.75,
+6 %/winter; parking × 1.25, streets × 1.4). Each listing expired 2–4 days later. A target was
"occupied" by *any* contract in `active` — completed and lost ones too — so a lot was listed at most
once a winter.

**Kinds.** One-time job (paid once when every box is complete); recurring (a visit whenever its
boxes reopen, paid per visit; renewable at winter's end); municipal (a street section, recurring,
the grader skips it — `contracted_segments()`; needed reputation 58 and a plow/blower vehicle).

**Bidding and rivals.** `place_bid` took the listing off the board; the answer came 1–3 game hours
later (`resolve_at`). `_resolve_bids` (hourly) compared the player's score — amount / budget ×
(1 − (rep − 50) / 400 − 0.08 near your clients ± equipment) — with the best of 2–3 rival draws
U(0.86, 1.14) + their edge (`_rng_for(lid × 11)`). No live state; the board showed "Odds: …" on a
±40 % slider.

**Reputation.** Starts at 50 (0–100). +2 per one-time job, +1 per visit done within 24 snowed-in
hours (+0.3 after), −6 per lost contract. Parking lots from 52 (or a blower / second vehicle),
streets from 58 + a plow/blower vehicle.

**The 48-hour clock** (`contract_snowed_in_hours`): `on_advance` added game minutes to
`snowed_min` while any box was incomplete, paused while heavy snow or worse fell
(`storming()`); warnings at 12 h and 3 h left; lost after 48 h. **Readers:** `hours_left()`; the
HUD alert line ("⚠ addr: N h left to clear") and box line ("Snowed in — N h left before X gives up
(clock paused: storm)"); the computer's Active tab ("Snowed in — N h left to clear it (paused while
the storm falls)") and the board's terms ("within 48 game hours", "Lost after 48 game hours snowed
in"); the map (house tag "Nh", legend "— N h left"); notifications ("…snowed in a long time — N
hours left", "URGENT: … lost in N hours", "CONTRACT LOST: … for over 48 hours"); the Help app; tests
(test_systems `…lost_after_48_hours…`, polish3's 90 % snap test, polish5 §7 history and §9 warning /
equality tests).

**Zone markers** (`zone_markers.gd`): for every incomplete zone of an active / needs-service contract
(not Gerry's trail): orange stakes with a flag at the four corners of each zone rect, an orange line
on the snow around each rect within 25 m of the player (redrawn every 0.5 s), and the box-complete
flash. Gerry's trail is the blue `BoxOutline` quest outline. Untouched here (§10b).

## 10.1 Tiers and reputation

- **Tiers** (`rep_tier_names` ["Residential", "Commercial", "City"], `rep_tier_thresholds` [0, 68, 85]).
  `Contracts.tier_index()`, `tier_name()`, `tier_threshold()`, `next_tier()`, `listing_tier(l)`,
  `listing_locked(l)`; a listing's tier comes from its target (`lot:` residential, `area:`
  commercial, `road:` / `walk:` city). The board shows every listing; above the tier it reads
  "Needs reputation N" and has no bid controls; `place_bid` refuses it. `municipal_unlocked()` /
  `big_jobs_unlocked()` now mean the tier (the old vehicle requirement for streets is gone — reading:
  Decision 5 makes it reputation).
- **Reaching a tier**: one notification ("Commercial tier reached (reputation 68): parking lot
  contracts are open to you."), once per tier (`tier_noted`, saved; derived from the reputation for
  an old save so nothing already reached is announced). Every reputation change of the contracts
  goes through `Contracts.adjust_reputation`.
- **Paths first.** Residential listings are a path job (`lotN_path`) or a driveway job
  (`lotN_driveway`: "today's residential zone" read as the driveway box). Driveway share
  `driveway_share()` = lerp(`driveway_share_rep0` 0.25, `driveway_share_commercial` 0.5) over 0 →
  the Commercial threshold, flat above. Reputation still starts at 50 (unchanged), where the share is
  0.43. Sidewalks are no longer part of residential jobs: they are **city sidewalk contracts**
  (`walk:<row>_<w|e>`, the three lots' sidewalk boxes along one side of a street, west or east of
  Cedar Lane, minus any box of an open residential job; named "Sidewalks, 11–19 Maple Street" by
  `Layout.walk_section_name`).
- **Gain** (`Contracts.rep_gain(size, visit)`): `rep_gain_base` 0.5 + `rep_gain_per_m3` 0.03 × the
  job's size (m³: the listing's estimate), at most `rep_gain_max` 2.0; a visit × `rep_visit_factor`
  1.0 (a visit is a job); above `rep_gain_full_below` 50 the gain falls off linearly towards 100
  (never under a tenth). A miss: `contract_lost_reputation` 6 (unchanged). Gerry's trail keeps +2.
- **Pacing projection** (`contract_bench.tscn -- --pacing=2`): the real board, rivals, award, due
  times and gains through whole winters, with a modelled good player — bids at the suggested price
  on one new one-time job a day it has room for, keeps 3 recurring clients a winter (6 in winter 2),
  finishes a fraction p of its jobs (the rest are missed), buys a blower at Commercial and a plow
  truck at City. Seeds 2024 / 7 / 99:

| p | Commercial reached | City reached |
|---|---|---|
| 0.95 | w1 Dec 24 / w1 Dec 28 / w1 Feb 5 | — (w2 ends 73) / w1 Mar 26 / w2 Jan 10 |
| **0.97** | **w1 Dec 24 / w1 Dec 28 / w1 Jan 9** | **w2 Jan 22 / w1 Feb 12 / w2 Mar 6** |
| 0.99 | w1 Dec 24 / w1 Dec 28 / w1 Dec 30 | w1 Feb 5 / w1 Feb 12 / w1 Feb 23 |

  A player who finishes ~97 % of their jobs reaches Commercial in winter 1 and City in winter 2 in
  two of three seeds (seed 7: late winter 1). ~100 one-time jobs and 60–190 visits a winter.
  Reputation is sensitive to misses by design (−6 against ~0.5–1 per job): a player missing one job
  in twenty plateaus in the 70s. Seed 2024 at 0.97: Dec 16 64, Jan 15 70, Mar 31 68; winter 2 Jan 15
  82, Feb 14 89 (`pacing_final.log`).
- **After every snowfall**, residential listings within reach: the projection's 65 mornings after a
  snowfall had min 1, median 4 open residential listings the player could take (`pacing_final.log`).

## 10.2 The bidding day

- **Each listing** (`_post`): `posted`, `closes` (`close_for(posted)`: 9 p.m. that day if posted at
  least 3 game hours before, else 9 p.m. the next day), `tier`, `job` (path / driveway / parking /
  street / sidewalk), `now_volume` (snow on it now), `budget` (= the suggested price), `bid` (yours,
  0 = none), `bid_at`, `closed`, `outbid_at`, `rivals` (the plan). Shown on the board: address, kind
  ("Path job · one-time"), client, boxes and area, the snow on it (m³), "closes 9 p.m. today", the
  number of bids, and your status.
- **Supply** (`_generate_listings`): every morning at 6:00 (and at winter start) the board is filled to
  `listings_per_day` 6 from the supply's own stream (`_supply_rng(day)`, new): city
  (`listing_share_city` 0.15: a street section or a sidewalk section, half each), commercial
  (`listing_share_commercial` 0.15: a parking lot), the rest residential — a lot with no running job
  and snow on the box, a driveway job with `driveway_share()`, else a path job; recurring
  `listing_recurring_share` 0.45 of the time (not on day 0). **Size cap** (feasibility, below): a
  residential job is posted only if its snow is at most what the player's best equipment clears in a
  day — `res_cap_m3()` = `listing_res_max_m3` 18 m³ × `EQUIP_RATE[capability] / EQUIP_RATE[0]`
  (`EQUIP_RATE` [0.02, 0.06, 0.1, 0.5] m³/s: the board's old minutes-estimate table, now one place);
  a driveway too big falls back to the same client's path. Completed / lost contracts no longer block
  their lot (only running ones do), so clients come back after new snow and missed jobs can be
  relisted. No streets / lots already bare are listed.
- **Bids** (`place_bid(lid, amount)`, `withdraw_bid(lid)`): until the listing closes; not under the
  floor `bid_floor(l)` = ⌈suggested × `bid_floor_frac` 0.8⌉ (everyone's floor); not on a locked
  listing. `Contracts.bids` is now derived (your live bids: `{lid, amount, at, listing}`), rebuilt by
  `_sync_bids()`, not saved. Notifications "Bid of $X placed on …", "Bid on … changed to $X.", "Bid on
  … withdrawn."
- **Rivals** (`_plan_rivals`, the rivals' own stream `_rival_rng(lid, salt)` — a pure function of the
  seed, winter, listing and decision, so replies are the same whenever they happen):
  `rival_count_min`–`max` 1–3 per listing (+1 above residential), each opening at
  suggested × U(`rival_open_min` 0.97, `rival_open_max` 1.2) × (1 + its edge) × (1.08 near your home
  or clients — `proximity_bid_bonus`, which keeps Gerry's "jobs near your other clients are easier
  to win" true), never under the floor, at a seeded time between an hour after posting and 1.5 h
  before the close. When your bid takes the lead from the rival who had it, that rival may answer
  (`rival_reply_chance` 0.5, at most `rival_replies_max` 2 per listing) 30–120 game minutes later,
  `rival_reply_step_min`–`max` 2–6 % under your bid, never under the floor; a tie goes to you.
- **Live status**: `bid_status(l)` → "Winning" / "Outbid · lowest bid $X"; `bid_count(l)`. The board
  rebuilds on `Contracts.changed` and every 2 s (the board joined `LIVE_APPS`). With the board not on
  screen, a rival bid that puts you behind sends "Outbid on 31 Birch Avenue — lowest bid $X." at most
  once per listing per game hour (`outbid_at`). Reading: "with the computer closed" taken as "unless
  the Contract board is on screen", so another app open doesn't swallow it.
- **Closing and award** (`on_advance` → `_close_step`): at 9:00 p.m. the listing is `closed`
  (bids refused, "Bidding closed — the result at 9:01 p.m."); at 9:01 p.m. the lowest bid wins (tie →
  you): "Bid won! Maple Snow Removal wins 15 Birch Avenue — one-time job at $41, due 9 p.m. Wed." /
  "Bid lost: 23 Birch Avenue — Polar Plow Co. won at $39."; `Clock.drop_to_1x("bid")` for each of
  yours. The contract's `accepted` is 9:01 p.m. exactly (any step size).
- **Ease** (`contract_bench.tscn -- --ease=40`: 40 boards × 3 seeds, 491 residential listings, the
  player bidding once at a seeded time of the day): at 100 % of the suggested price **69.7 %** won,
  at 95 % 72.5 %, at 90 % 71.7 % (the rivals answer undercuts), bidding 100 % and adjusting to the
  lowest − $1 when outbid **99.8 %** (the floor always wins). Rivals answered 536 undercuts.
- **Typing a bid**: the board's amount field is a `LineEdit` (`ComputerUI.bid_edits`), so
  `Game.typing()` holds every game key while it has focus (tested with T, G, 2); Enter places the bid;
  Esc leaves any text field of the computer (§7's `_input`, generalised).

## 10.3 Due times (Decision 4)

- **One-time job**: due 9 p.m. the day after it was won (`_set_due_after_award`; a 9:01 p.m. award →
  9 p.m. the next day; `force_award` at other times → 9 p.m. the next day).
- **Recurring / city visit**: when the boxes reopen the visit is open with its due time *pending*
  (`due_pending(c)`); each step while snow falls records it; once it stops, due = 9 p.m. the day after
  the snowfall ended (`_deadlines_step`). A recurring contract's first visit (won with snow on it) is
  due like a one-time job.
- **Storms**: heavy snow or worse falling inside a job's window moves its due time one day, once per
  job / visit (`ext`), with "Storm: … is now due 9 p.m. Thu." A visit's window opens when the boxes
  reopen, so a storm that reopens a client gives that visit its day too (reading: this is the
  replacement for the old storm pause; without it every recurring client is due by 9 p.m. the day
  after a 1 m major event). This replaces HANDOFF §13 decision (c) and item 19.
- **Warnings** at `contract_warn_hours` 12 and `contract_last_warn_hours` 3 before the due time
  ("31 Birch Avenue is due 9 p.m. Tue — 12 h left to clear it.", "URGENT: … — 3 h left."),
  `Clock.drop_to_1x("contract")`.
- **Done** = every box complete before the due time (box completion fires `_refresh` → `_finish`):
  paid, reputation +gain, history "completed". A job done at 8:59 p.m. is paid; snow after completion
  doesn't touch a finished job.
- **Missed** (`now ≥ due`, so at 9:00 p.m. exactly): reputation −6, no pay, history "missed",
  "MISSED: … wasn't cleared by 9 p.m. — no pay, -6 reputation. It's back on the board.",
  `drop_to_1x("contract")`, and `_relist(c)`: a fresh listing for the same client and boxes, closing
  at the next 9 p.m. (a 9:00 p.m. miss → 9 p.m. the next day), with its own rivals.
- **Everything on the clock**: rivals, close, award, due times, warnings and misses run in
  `Contracts.on_advance` (the per-step tick; fast-forward runs it per substep). Order in a step: a
  jump guard (`_rejump`, below), the rivals' bids due, closing / award, due times.
- **Payment exactly once**: one-time → `completed` (never paid again); a visit → `serviced` until its
  boxes reopen (a new visit); tested by re-measuring, an hourly refresh and new snow on a finished
  job.
- **Shown** (every reader of the old timer): HUD alert line "⚠ 15 Birch Avenue: due 9 p.m. Wed — 1 h
  left"; HUD box line's second line "Due 9 p.m. Wed  (1 h left)" (format §10b's); computer Contracts
  → Active "Due 9 p.m. Wed  ·  14 h left  ·  a storm added a day"; the map's house tag "due Wed" (the
  hour is always 9 p.m.; "due 9 p.m. Wed" didn't fit under the number on the lot) and legend "• addr
  (to do — due 9 p.m. Wed)"; notifications above; the Help app's line; the board's header line.
  `Contracts.due_text(c)`, `when_text(m)`, `when_text_rel(m)`, `clock_text(m)`, `MapUI.due_tag(c)`.
  `contract_snowed_in_hours` is removed from the balance data; `snowed_min` stays in old contracts
  (unused).
- **Jump guard** (`_rejump`): a listing still open whose close passed before the current step began
  (the clock was *set* past it — tests, shots; play, sleep and F1 jumps always step through 9 p.m.)
  is posted again from now, so `Clock.reset` in a test doesn't empty the board.

## 10.4 Feasibility — `tests/contract_bench.tscn`

A bot clears a box with the real controls (look at the nearest snow in the box, hold the primary
button and sweep until full, turn to the side the game's own throw — `ToolController._landing`, walls
included — lands farthest clear of the box, release), seed 2024. "Human pace" counts 1.1 s per
stroke plus the walking (as `grind_bench`). The day's window 7 a.m.–9 p.m. = 14 game h = 17.5 real
min.

**The brief's four runs** — a typical job after a typical storm: every residential path and driveway
cleared the evening before winter 1's first storm (Dec 4, 8 p.m.), then the storm (Dec 5, 21.7 cm)
and the grader's pass to Dec 6, 7 a.m.; the median box of each kind (`bench_storm.log`):

| Job | Tool | Snow | Bot | Human pace | Fits 14 game h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| path (16 Birch Ave; 1.8–2.6 m³) | small shovel | 2.2 m³ | 1.3 min (1.0 game h) | **1.8 min (1.4 game h)** | yes, 12.6 h to spare |
| path | standard shovel | 2.2 m³ | 1.0 min (0.8 game h) | 1.4 min (1.1 game h) | yes |
| driveway (20 Birch Ave; 14.0–18.2 m³) | small shovel | 15.8 m³ | 8.8 min (7.0 game h) | **12.0 min (9.6 game h)** | yes, 4.4 h to spare |
| driveway | standard shovel | 15.8 m³ | 6.6 min (5.2 game h) | 9.0 min (7.2 game h) | yes |

**Untouched lots** (`--state=board`: the same morning, the boxes as a lot nobody cleared holds them —
the whole season's snow): a path (11.8 m³, 10.1–14.1) with the small shovel **7.5 min (6.0 game h)**
— fits; the standard shovel's bot run stalled at 89 % on the porch steps (a bot limitation, 26.6 min);
a driveway (92.1 m³, 82–105) with the small shovel: **37 % in 18.8 min (15.0 game h)**, i.e. about 2.7
days of work — never a one-day job with hand tools. Untouched residential boxes over winter 1 (seed
2024, median of 20 lots): path 8.6 m³ (Dec 2) → 11.8 (Dec 8) → 14.8 (Jan 1) → 29.0 (Jan 22, after the
major event) → 38.0 (Mar 21); driveway 60 → 96 → 122 → 225 → 288 m³.

**So the size of early listings is tuned, not the tools**: `listing_res_max_m3` = 18 m³ (the largest
"after a typical storm" driveway, 18.2 m³, at about 11 game hours) caps residential listings while
the best tool is a shovel; better equipment scales it (`EQUIP_RATE`: ×3 pushers, ×5 blowers, ×25 a
plow/blower vehicle). With a shovel the board offers paths (untouched ones until the January major
event, then former clients' after new snow) and the driveways of clients cleared before; untouched
driveways come with a blower (early winter) or a vehicle. The driveway share is the draw; the cap
decides whether that client's driveway is within reach (else the client's path is listed). In the
pacing projection, mornings after a snowfall always had a residential listing within reach (min 1,
median 4).

## 10.5 Old saves (version 3 unchanged; new keys optional)

`Contracts._migrate_due_times()` on load fills in what's missing, nothing else: an active one-time
job without `due` → 9 p.m. on the first day at least 24 game hours after the load; a pending
recurring / city visit → opened at the load (due 9 p.m. the day after the snow now falling stops);
jobs above the tier are kept and run (`tier`, `job`, `size` derived); listings without `closes` →
posted now, close at the next 9 p.m., rivals planned from now; a pending bid of the old kind (its
listing off the board, `resolve_at`) → the listing back on the board with your bid on it, closing at
the next 9 p.m. `tier_noted` defaults to the current tier. Saves stay **version 3**; the new keys
are inside `contracts`: `tier_noted`; per listing `posted`, `closes`, `closed`, `tier`, `job`,
`now_volume`, `bid`, `bid_at`, `outbid_at`, `relisted`, `rivals[{r, at, open, bid, replies, next,
next_step}]`; per contract `due`, `ext`, `reopened`, `snow_seen`, `tier`, `job`, `size`,
`suggested`. `contracts.bids` is no longer written (derived). Every pass-3/4/5 fixture loads and
plays (the fixture tests).

## Readings taken

- **Reputation 0** in the share rule is the bottom of the 0–100 scale; the game still starts at 50
  (unchanged), so a new company draws 43 % driveways — but with a shovel the size cap turns most of
  them into paths (driveways untouched all season are 60–290 m³).
- **"Typical driveway job after a typical storm"** = a driveway cleared before the storm and holding
  that storm's snow plus the grader's apron windrow — the only kind a hand tool finishes in a day;
  the cap keeps the board to those (and untouched paths). Equipment raises the cap (the existing
  board estimate table), so a player with a blower gets untouched driveways.
- **City work is reputation only** (Decision 5); the old "plow or blower vehicle" condition for
  streets is gone. City sidewalk sections are the lots' sidewalk boxes in rows of three (the only
  sidewalk boxes the map has).
- **A visit's window** opens when its boxes reopen, so the storm that reopened them counts for the
  storm day.
- **"With the computer closed"** → unless the board is on screen.
- **Gains**: the brief's "if there's no gain today, add one" — there was one; it is now scaled with
  size (the coordinator's list) and falls off near the top so City lands in winter 2 for a good
  player. Visits gain like jobs (`rep_visit_factor` 1.0).
- **Everyone's floor** is one balance fraction (`bid_floor_frac`), for you and the rivals; ties go to
  you, so bidding at the floor always wins.
- **Relisting** a missed recurring / city contract puts the whole contract back on the board (the
  client leaves).
- **Completed lots** can be listed again (only running contracts block a target): needed for
  relisting and for former clients after new snow.
- **The tutorial job** keeps its +2.
- `Contracts.storming()` stays (unused by the UI now).
