## 6.1 The models

Kept exactly, so nothing that holds, places or measures a tool moves: the tool frame (origin = the
top hand's grip, −Z forward), `tip()` (where the shaft meets the blade; the 55° shaft), `blade_offset()`
(the load), the blade frame of the first-person blade part (origin at the tip, floor at y = 0), each
blade's width, the handle / blade split, `_grip_frames()`, the blowers' collision box and chute pivot
(0, 0.62, −0.25). Only geometry and materials changed.

(tubes along a smoothed path: D-grips, the U-bar, handle loops, chutes, lips), `_sheet` (a thin curved
sheet from a profile: scoops, faces, moldboards, housings, deflectors; its UV runs from the edge that
scrapes, for edge wear), `_wall` / `_side_plate` (side walls and housing plates), `_strip` (wear strips
and cutting edges with rivets), `_rivet`, `_wheel` (tyre, hub, optional lugs), `_helix` (auger paddles
and flights), `_spline` (Hermite segments with chord-scaled tangents, so a short corner next to a long
straight never overshoots — found when the sleigh's lip bulged 1.2 cm out of its width).

## 6.2 Materials

`shaders/tool.gdshader` (world, third person, the photo, the rack, the icons) and
`shaders/viewmodel_clip.gdshader` (first person; its under-the-snow clip and white rim unchanged) share
`shaders/tool_surface.gdshaderinc`. `Mats.tool(finish, colour, roughness, metallic, wear, edge_wear,
axis)`; finishes `ToolModels.FIN_*`:

- **plastic** — satin, faint mottling, whitened scuffs and fine scratches (stress whitening), more along
  a blade's front edge;
- **aluminium** — brushed streaks along the shaft, darker scratches;
- **painted steel** — chips showing bare metal, more on the edges;
- **bare steel** — scraped bright scratches running fore and aft, a faint rust tint elsewhere (strips,
  cutting edges, rivets, ferrules);
- **wood** — grain along the shaft, varnish sheen, a darker handled patina;
- **fibreglass** — glossy with faint fibres along the axis;
- **rubber / foam** — matte with fine pores.

Finish 0 is the old flat colour (a `StandardMaterial3D` given to `_clip_mat` still converts as before).
Reading: metallic values are kept moderate (aluminium 0.45, steel 0.5, paint 0.05): this renderer has
little to reflect, and at 0.85–0.9 the shafts and edges rendered nearly black and the painted
moldboards muddy (first look, `shots/sc/v_00.png`).

- **Load:** `blade_offset()` unchanged; the load point lies inside each scoop / tub (above the floor,

- **Clip:** every first-person surface goes through `viewmodel_clip` (tested); the gameplay test of the

- **Blade widths:** each blade (the blade part's width) is the profile's `width` within 1.5 cm (the

## 6.4 Icons

`scripts/ui/tool_icons.gd` (`ToolIcons`): the first `Icons.tool` that draws asks for the set; a node
under the root builds one `SubViewport` (own world, transparent background, 1920 × 768 px) with one
orthographic camera looking from the front left, above (`VIEW`), a key, a fill and a rim light, and one
model per icon (the mitten, then every tool and machine in `Catalog.TOOL_IDS` order), each framed in its
own square (cells 1.6 squares apart so a handle running off a corner never reaches the next icon); it
builds with drawing off, waits a frame, draws once (as the photo does since §4), reads the image back,
cuts each square, scales it to 128 px (Lanczos), adds mipmaps, and frees the viewport. Long hand tools
are turned about the view axis (so the angle they are seen from is the same) until their grip-to-blade
line runs down to the left, and framed from the blade up 80 % of the shaft so the blade is large at
48–72 px. Every icon is drawn over the same soft light backdrop (`Icons.backdrop()`), so dark handles
read on the dark panels. Headless — and in the frames before the render finishes — `Icons` draws the
old procedural icons; no test reads a captured image.

Used by: the hotbar (70 px), the store (72 px; tools and blowers), the upgrades (72 px; reading: a
hand-tool upgrade shows the small shovel, a blower upgrade the small blower — the kind of item it applies
to; vehicle, clothing, weather and property upgrades have no tool icon), the inventory panel (48 px).
The rack is §15's. Bare hands: a rendered mitten (the character's mittens' blue-grey, lightened to read
on the slots). Empty slots: the hotbar's dimming is unchanged.
