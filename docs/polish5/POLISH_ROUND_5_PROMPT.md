## Decisions (already made — apply them)

1. **Snow from inside the house.** The playtester could shovel the outdoor snow from the bedroom window. That's a bug. From inside any building you can't scoop, push, throw or blow outdoor snow, whether through a window, a wall or an open door (§3).
2. **Keys** (§2, §9).
   - `drop` moves from Q to **G**. What it does is unchanged.
   - A new `store` action on **F** puts the item in your hands on a rack (§15).
   - `interact` stays on **E** for doors, vehicles and everything else.
   - `use_pouch` loses G and moves to **H**, or to the nearest free key if H is taken (report which).
   - Fast-forward gets one rebindable action that cycles 1× → 2× → 10× → 1×. Its default is **T** if T is free.
3. **Day length** (§9). One game day lasts 30 real minutes: 1 real minute = 48 game minutes (24 today).
   - **World rates keep their game-time values:** weather and snowfall, the city's schedule, store hours, bills, contract deadlines, loose-snow hardening, house cooling.
   - **Rates the player feels while playing are recalibrated** so that at 1× they feel the same per real minute as now: warmth lost and regained, and the heating pouch. They still run on game time, so sleep and fast-forward apply them in full.
   - **Real-time things are unchanged:** walking, digging, filling, throwing, animations.
4. **Contract deadlines replace the 48-hour rule** (§10).
   - **The cycle.** Bidding on a listing closes at 9:00 p.m. At 9:01 p.m. the lowest bid wins. The job is due at 9:00 p.m. the next day.
   - **Recurring and city contracts.** A visit falls due when the contract's boxes reopen. It is due by 9:00 p.m. on the day after the snowfall that reopened them ends.
   - **Storms.** If heavy snow or worse falls inside a job's window, its deadline moves one day later, once per job, with a notification. This replaces the storm pause (decision (c) in HANDOFF §13).
   - **A missed job** costs the existing reputation loss and pays nothing. The contract goes back on the board, and the player may bid on it again.
   - **Gerry's trail** has no deadline.
5. **Reputation tiers** (§10).
   - **Residential** from the start. Residential jobs come in two kinds: a **path job** (the front walk, from the sidewalk to the door) and a **driveway job** (today's residential zone). Paths are easier, so at low reputation most listings are path jobs, and the share of driveway jobs grows with reputation.
   - **Commercial** (the school, store and church parking lots) at a first threshold.
   - **City** (street sections and sidewalks) at a second threshold.

   Listings above the player's tier are shown locked, with the reputation they need. City sidewalk contracts cover sidewalk boxes that aren't part of an open residential job.

   Pacing target: a player who completes most of their jobs reaches Commercial during winter 1 and City during winter 2.

   Contracts already held in an old save run until they end, even above the player's tier.
6. **City crews on Mondays and Fridays** (§11).
   - **The grader** passes every Monday and Friday, instead of every 3–4 days. Only its schedule changes. Its pass stays exactly as it is: how it drives, its lane op, and the rough job it leaves, with the little accumulation and the bank in the middle of the street. The playtester likes it that way.
   - **A new sidewalk crew** follows the grader on the same days and clears every sidewalk to bare pavement (100 %). It leaves the snow as a mound on the strip between the sidewalk and the street.
   - Grader windrows across driveway aprons stay.
   - City contracts (streets and sidewalks) are extra clearing the city pays for between passes.
   - The sidewalk crew skips any sidewalk section that has an open player job. The grader runs its whole route, as it does now.
7. **Forecast surprises stay, but they're fair** (§8).
   - Forecast events: the TV names the right strength (light snowfall, storm, major event) from three days out.
   - A storm that arrives stronger than forecast is called at its real strength on the TV's "today" card from 7:00 a.m. on its day, and on the radio within its window.
   - An unforecast major event still never shows on the TV before it starts, but the radio picks it up 3 game hours ahead (`radio_surprise_horizon_h` 1 → 3, the whole radio window).
8. **Upgrades belong to an item type** (§13). If you own two small shovels, they share the small shovel's upgrades. Upgrades bought in an old save carry over to every item they used to affect, so nothing the player paid for is lost.
9. **Fun Mode** has no cold. The coat isn't sold there, the cold-night hint never shows, and cab heat doesn't matter. Everything else in this round applies to both modes (§13, §14).
10. **The commercial property** replaces 24 and 28 Maple Street with one property at 24 Maple Street (§16).
    - Lot ids are never renumbered.
    - Price it so that a player who does well can buy it in winter 3 or 4.
    - Before the purchase it's locked, with a "For sale" sign. Afterwards the sign shows the company name.
11. **The garage door** opens only from its button inside the garage. You reach the garage through the house (§15).
12. **The road rule is removed** (§3). Players need somewhere to put the snow, and the playtester wants more chaos. Snow from yards may go onto the street, whether it's thrown, pushed, blown or plowed. It lands where it was aimed, with volume conserved, and stays there until the grader's next pass (Decision 6).

- **Audit first:** what happens to warmth in a vehicle today, and how much more warmth a night costs than a day now.

- **Heated cabs:**
  - inside an enclosed vehicle with the engine running, the player warms up (a balance rate, like indoors but slower)
  - with the engine off, the cab keeps the wind out but doesn't warm
  - open machines give no shelter
  - vehicles without an enclosed cab can get a cab upgrade where it makes sense (§13)
- **The hint.** At dusk, when the forecast low (§8 — not the truth) is under a balance threshold: "Cold night ahead. Sleep, or work from a heated cab." Once a night, in the HUD speech box, and not when the player is already indoors or in a heated cab.
- The exposure curve, the coat and Fun Mode behave as §9 and §13 say.

**Done when / tests**
- Warmth lost at night is greater than by day, at the same wind chill.
- A heated cab with the engine on warms the player; with it off, it shelters; an open machine doesn't.
- The hint shows once a night.
