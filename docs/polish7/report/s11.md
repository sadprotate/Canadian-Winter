# Polish 7 — §11 Blade up/down, push and pull

## What changed

- **The actions** (`settings.gd`):
  - `veh_blade_up` "Raise the blade" (Up) and `veh_blade_down` "Lower the blade" (Down), in the
    vehicle context right after `veh_tool`, so the settings menu and the Help app list them beside
    the blade toggle.
  - Both are in `RATE_LIMITED`.
  - No `CONTROLS_VERSION` bump: existing players get them from the migration. If a player already
    has a vehicle or global action on Up or Down, the new one starts unbound.
  - Conflicts: none in any place (`test_s2_no_conflict_in_any_context` and `test_s11_the_actions`).
- **What they do** (`vehicle.gd`):
  - `_input` reads them before the GUI, checking `occupied`, `has_blade()`, `Game.input_captured()`
    and `Game.typing()`, and sends each press through `Settings.accept_press()`.
  - `set_blade(down)` changes the blade only when it isn't that way already. Through `_toggle_tool()`
    that gives the click and "Blade down" / "Blade up".
  - `has_blade()` is a plow with no dump bed: the pickup with plow, the ATV, the tractor with blade,
    the plow truck and the store's grader. On the plain pickup, the blower vehicles and the dump truck
    the keys do nothing.
  - `veh_tool` (left mouse) still toggles. The blade still animates between its two heights.
- **The focus problem:**
  - The arrows are also `ui_up` / `ui_down`, and a focused control takes them as a focus move before
    `_unhandled_input` would see them. Reading them in `_input` and marking them handled fixes that.
  - The test focuses a HUD button with a neighbour below it. On foot, Down moves its focus. In the
    truck, Down lowers the blade and the focus stays.
  - (The persistent HUD buttons, fast-forward, were already `FOCUS_NONE`.)
- **Back-drag** (blade down, reversing): `back_drag()` calls `Snow.blade_push` with the face turned
  toward the vehicle.
  - The band is the reverse travel + 0.1 m, the snow lands `DRAG_CAST` 0.3 m behind the blade, and the
    guard is on the uncovered side.
  - §9's rules hold: the spread band, `soft` marks, one `push_rng` draw per moved cell. No global
    `randf()`: the powder burst stays on the push path. Volume is exact.
  - A cast of 0.3 m keeps the snow against the back face (0.1–0.62 m) and clear of every blade
    vehicle's front wheels. The ATV's are the closest, 1 m behind its blade.
  - Status "Blade down — dragging" while it drags. It also replaces "Blade down", but not a blocked
    reason. `hud_text()` adds "— dragging".
- **Two things found while testing, fixed:**
  - *Standing still.* The brief: "Standing still adds nothing new". After a drag, the stopped truck ran
    today's standstill push. That push takes the snow within 0.1 m behind the face, which is the
    dragged pile now, and throws it 0.6 m forward. It also moves the guard to the pile's side. So the
    pile went back through the blade. Measured: 1.1 m³ of a 4.2 m³ pile back on the door side.
    - Now the blade holds the side it last worked (`_drag_side`). After a push, and whenever the blade
      has just been lowered, it is today's push band. After a drag, it is the drag's band on the
      cleared ground, which moves nothing and keeps the guard there.
    - Pushing is unchanged: the push path runs exactly as before whenever the vehicle last moved
      forward.
  - *The slowdown.* A push's berm lowers `max_fwd`. In reverse the limit is
    `min(reverse_speed, max_fwd × 0.6 + 1)`, so the berm only slowed a reverse above about 3 m³ (the
    test: 17.0 km/h dragging against 17.2 bare).
    - While dragging, the reverse limit is now also capped at `reverse_speed × work_drag`: the same
      factor a push gets, nothing else changed.
    - With 2.4 m³ against the door: 15.5 km/h dragging, 17.2 bare.
- **Help:**
  - The first-drive panel (`panels.gd`) adds two lines under the blade toggle: "<Up> / <Down>  raise
    / lower the blade" and "<reverse>, blade down  drag the snow back". The labels come from
    `action_label`.
  - The Help app's Controls table lists every action in order, so it shows both keys beside the blade
    toggle with no change.

## Review (four reviewers, each finding checked by a skeptic)

Four findings were confirmed and fixed. All the tests above were rerun after the fixes (polish7,
polish5 `test_s13_`, gameplay `vehicle_plow` / `plow_pushes` / `truck_keys`, polish6 `test_s9_`,
polish5 `s3_road_rule`).

1. **Driving forward off a dragged pile carried part of it away.** The brief says the pile stays when
   you drive forward.
   - Cause 1: the push's 0.1 m margin reached back into the pile's nearest row. Cause 2: when the
     guard changed sides, the pile's steep front slumped into the gap the blade opened, and the blade
     carried that snow off.
   - While leaving a drag (`_leaving_drag`), the push now takes only the ground crossed since the
     drag stopped, half a cell clear of the stop line. Its guard reaches a cell past the face. Once
     the blade is a push's margin past the line, it pushes exactly as before.
   - Every other push is unchanged: the same band and guard arguments.
   - New test: `test_s11_driving_off_the_pile`.
2. **The help line was too wide.** At 74 characters it pushed the panel off the right edge, so the
   back-drag hint was cut off. It is now two short lines, and the test checks the panel fits on the
   screen.
3. **`test_s13_ownership_and_the_front_plow` had lost its "one truck" check.** Restored through a
   lookup: after the old save's order is fitted, `Game.vehicles.size() == nveh + 1` and every truck is
   plowed.
4. **`test_s13_upgrades_app` compared the app with itself** (its expected rows were filtered by
   `track_shown`). It now builds the expected rows as before, from each item's tracks with the plain
   pickup's card taking the plowed pickup's, and leaves out only `front_plow`.

Rejected by the skeptics:

- `make_fixtures.gd` still assumes the plain pickup: it is a manual tool, and the committed fixtures
  are unchanged.
- The street-lock tests use an empty vehicle list rather than the plain pickup: equivalent, since the
  plain pickup has no plow.
- The §11 tests read the live bindings and could break on a player's custom file: hypothetical.
