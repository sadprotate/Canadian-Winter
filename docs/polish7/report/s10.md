# Polish 7 — §10 The plow truck from the start, good in snow

## What changed

- **A new game spawns `pickup_plow`** at `Game.truck_start()`, snowed in as before
  (`game_state.gd`). The shovel and the keys are unchanged.
  - The blade fits where the plain pickup stood, so the truck isn't moved. At ±30° the blade stays
    inside the driveway, clear of the sidewalk and the garage. Nearest distances: the buried shovel
    2.5 m, the mailbox 8 m, the keys 13 m.
- **The keys lock it** (`Vehicle.is_pickup()`: `PICKUP` or `PICKUP_PLOW`): "[E] Pickup truck (locked)"
  and the locked message, as before.
- **Good in snow** (`vehicle.gd`):
  - Its wheels are the pickup's: `stuck_depth()` is `pickup_stuck_depth`, blade up or down, and so is
    the "Stuck: … only clears about N cm" line.
  - With the blade up, the round 5 §12 branch runs for it: drag scale, shove, push-back, bog, wall, and
    their messages.
  - With the blade down, it runs the plow path as before.
  - No balance value changed.
- **The shove lands clear of the path.** The V already spans the truck's width, but its 1.5 m cast was
  set for the plain pickup's 2 m. On the plowed truck's 2.4 m it left 23% of the shoved snow in the
  truck's own path. `_shove` now casts `SHOVE_CAST × width / SHOVE_CAST_WIDTH`. For the plain pickup
  that is exactly 1.5 m, unchanged.
  - Measured over five lane offsets, the share inside the path is 2.5% for the plowed truck and 2.2%
    for the plain pickup.
  - Nothing lands on or behind the raised blade.
- **The front plow isn't sold.**
  - `ItemUpgrades.track_shown` hides `front_plow`, and `why_not` gives "No longer sold." for it.
  - The track stays in the catalog: old saves keep their granted plow and still read "Already owned.",
    and the legacy migration still works.
  - In the Upgrades app the truck's card is now the plowed truck's own, "Pickup Truck with Front
    Plow", with Plow width and Plow power. The plain pickup had nothing else to sell, so it has no
    card.
  - "Fit the front plow first" is gone from `why_not` and `computer_ui._not_owned_hint`. Those lines
    only appear in an old save that still has the plain pickup; they now say "You don't have this
    truck." and "—".
  - The store's fitting code is untouched. An old save's open order still gets fitted, which
    `test_s13_ownership_and_the_front_plow` checks.
  - The Equipment store never listed the plow (`Catalog.store_vehicles()`).
- **Contracts** (`contracts.gd`):
  - `equipment_capability()` skips `Kind.PICKUP_PLOW`, so the starting truck counts as hand tools.
  - `listing_needs_vehicle()` now checks `Game.vehicles` for any plow or blower vehicle itself, not
    `capability < 3`, so the starting truck opens street sections at the City tier.
  - `tests/contract_bench.gd` gives a good player the `plow_truck` at City, as its comment says,
    instead of a `pickup_plow` that no longer counts.
- **Showing the truck:** the framed photo and the menu's home shot build `pickup_plow` and add its
  blade raised, as a parked truck holds it.
  - In the menu the shoveller stood 0.4 m in front of the blade, so he moved 0.8 m toward the street.
- **Text:** the Help line and Gerry's keys line still read right for a truck with its plow: still
  locked, still snowed in. They are unchanged.
