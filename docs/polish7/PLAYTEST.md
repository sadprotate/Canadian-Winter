# Polish 7 — playtest checklist

Try these in **Fun Mode**, then repeat the truck checks (1–5) in **Survival Mode**.

## The truck has its plow (§10)

1. Start a new game. Outside, the truck in the driveway has a yellow plow on the front, raised.
   - Try to get in (E): it says the truck is locked and the keys are missing.
   - The framed photo by the front door shows the truck with its plow.
   - The main menu's view of the house shows the plow too.
2. Find the keys by the tree on the front lawn and get in. Getting in shouldn't say the truck is
   snowed in (getting out of the driveway: see "Playtest fix 1" below). Later, with the driveway
   cleared, drive out with the blade up: it should cross the windrow the city's grader leaves across
   the end of the driveway, as the old pickup did.
3. Drive the truck into deep snow and into a bank with the blade up. It should go as far as the old
   plain pickup did:
   - it shoves the snow ahead and out to the sides, clear of its own path;
   - it slows the deeper the snow;
   - it bogs down in snow too deep to push from a standstill. The message says to back up with the
     reverse key and take a run at it. Do that.
4. Press **Down** to lower the blade and push a windrow or a driveway's snow. Press **Up** to raise it.
   - Left mouse still lowers and raises it too.
   - Z / C still angle it.
5. Check the computer:
   - The Equipment store and the Upgrades app no longer offer a front plow.
   - The Upgrades app shows the truck's own Plow width and Plow power upgrades, on one card.
   - Nothing says "Fit the front plow first".
6. On day one the contract board's job sizes should look like before: the truck doesn't make the jobs
   bigger. Later, at the City reputation tier, street sections shouldn't say "Needs a plow or a
   blower vehicle".

## Push and pull (§11)

7. Drive up to the garage door (or the house wall) with the blade up. Lower it (Down) and back away
   (S):
   - The snow at the door should come away with the blade.
   - The status line should read "Blade down — dragging".
   - The truck shouldn't get stuck on its own pile.
   - With a lot of snow, the truck should back up a bit slower.
8. Raise the blade (Up) or drive forward: the snow stays where it is as a pile. Then back up past it and
   push it away with the blade down.
   - Stop while dragging with the blade still down: the pile should stay where it is, not creep back
     toward the door.
   - Drive forward with the blade still down after a drag: the pile should stay behind.
   - Raise the blade after a drag, then lower it again without moving: say whether the pile's nearest
     edge gets thrown forward. This is how a standing blade behaved before this round, and it was
     left that way.
9. Try Up and Down in the other blade vehicles bought from the store: the ATV, the tractor with blade,
   the plow truck and the grader. They should do nothing in the blower vehicles and the dump truck
   (Down must not dump the bed).
10. Click a HUD button (for example the fast-forward buttons) on foot, then get in the truck and press
    Down / Up. The blade should still move.

## Keys (§11)

11. Settings → Controls → Vehicles & machines: "Raise the blade" (Up) and "Lower the blade" (Down) sit
    under "Blade / blower on-off".
    - Rebind them to other keys and use them in the truck. The old keys should do nothing.
    - The truck's first-drive help panel shows the new keys.
    - Restore defaults puts them back on Up and Down.
12. If you had moved another vehicle action onto Up or Down before this build, the new action starts
    unbound ("—") instead of clashing.

## Playtest fix 1: the truck gets out of the driveway (re-check)

13. Start a new game, find the keys and get in. Shovel nothing.
    - Getting in says nothing about the truck being snowed in.
    - S backs it up (about 1 m, to the garage) and W moves it forward.
14. Blade down, the main way: press Down and drive forward out of the driveway to the street.
    - It starts slow (about 2 km/h) while its wheels are in the deep snow, then picks up once they
      run on what the blade has cleared.
    - It should reach the street in one push (about 9 s). If you stop and back up, raise the blade
      first (Up), or it drags the snow back with you.
    - The driveway's snow ends up as a big pile on the street in front of the blade. Say whether
      that's OK.
15. Blade up, the slower way: start another new game and drive out with the blade up.
    - It pushes off slowly (about 4 km/h). About 4 m in it stops: "Snowbank ahead … too much to push
      from a standstill … Back up with S…".
    - Back up all the way to the garage and drive at it again. Each run gets a little further, and
      about the fifth gets it out (about 45 s in all). A short back-up isn't enough: the deep snow
      where the truck was parked slows the run.
16. Make sure it still feels like deep snow: slow in the opening's snow, the plow slowed by its pile,
    and a bank above the grille still a wall ("…too much to drive through…").
17. Repeat 13–15 in Survival Mode.
18. Start another new game and dig out only around the truck's wheels with the shovel, not the
    driveway. Blade up, it now stops at once ("…too much to push from a standstill… Back up…"), where
    without digging it pushes off slowly. Back up and take a run at it. Say whether that feels right.
