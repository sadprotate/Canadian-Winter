# Polish 8 — playtest checklist

Play in **Survival Mode** unless a line says otherwise, then repeat the garage, hint and weather
checks in **Fun Mode**. F1 is the developer menu (weather, time, money).

## The opening, colder (§14)

1. Start a new game in Survival and play the opening without ever going indoors: the letter, dig
   the shovel out, Gerry's job, his trail.
   - By the time Gerry's trail is done (around midday at a steady pace) the cold warnings are
     showing: you should feel close to too cold to stay out, but not frozen.
   - The first cold warning also opens a "warming up" hint (once per game).
   - Gerry walks you in to his fire: it warms you back up.
2. Your house still warms you as fast as before; a heating pouch still helps outdoors.
3. Try a different seed or two (a mild first day and a colder one): it should be cold by the end
   of the trail either way.

## The hint panel (§17)

4. Talk to Gerry about the job: his snow tips come up in the panel at the top right. Use Back, Next
   and OK (the Left / Right / X keys, and clicking them when the cursor is free). The game never
   pauses for it.
5. The first time you drive the truck, its hint opens (driving, the blade keys, the engine key).
   The first time you pick up each tool, its hint opens.
6. Settings → Gameplay → "Show hints" off: no hint opens (Gerry says his tips aloud instead).

## The garage (§15)

7. The door from the kitchen to the garage is locked until you find the truck keys ("Locked: the
   keys are missing."). Find the keys: it opens.
8. Inside, press the button: the overhead door opens; press again: it closes (not while you stand in
   the doorway).
9. Drive the truck in and back out: blade up, blade down, angled left and right, with and without
   the plow width upgrade. A tractor stops at the door ("Too big for the garage.").
10. Sleep with the truck in the garage: it's still there in the morning. Leave it on the street and
    sleep: it's back in the driveway.
11. The rack: hold a tool and press F to hang it; look at it and press E to take it back.
12. No snow gets in: back-drag snow away from the closed door with the blade; push snow at the open
    door with the truck and a pusher; throw shovelfuls at it. None should land inside.

## The weather (§7)

13. With F1, go through Light, Moderate, Heavy and Blizzard, at noon and at night (F1 → time). Each
    should read at a glance:
    - light: pretty six-armed flakes drifting down, a few big soft ones close by; at night they
      glint near the street lamps and porch lights;
    - moderate: clearly more, a little faster;
    - heavy: dense and slanted, the end of the street gone, the wind louder;
    - blizzard: near whiteout, streaks driven sideways, gusts (with gust sounds), snow blowing along
      the ground and off the roofs, the trees swaying, a howling wind.
14. Watch from outdoors, from the living-room window and from the open garage door: snow falls
    outside, never inside the house or the garage.
15. F1 → "Windy (no snow)": wisps of snow run along the ground and off the roofs, with the wind
    sound; nothing falls.
16. Graphics → Particle density Low: less snow; High looks like Medium.
17. Check the box outlines (blue, money green) and the shovel's ring stay easy to see in a
    blizzard, at noon and at night.

## A cold night (§14)

18. On a cold day (F1: a January day, or wait for one), be outdoors at dusk: "Cold night ahead.
    Sleep, or work from a heated cab." shows once. If you're indoors at dusk, it shows the first time
    you go out that night.
19. Stay out on foot after dark: warmth drops faster than by day.
20. Get in the truck: with the engine running (I) the meter rises (slower than indoors); press I to
    turn it off: it stops rising and falls slowly; the truck won't drive and the blade keys do
    nothing until you press I again. The four-wheeler and the walk-behind blowers give no shelter;
    the garage shelters.
21. Sleeping through a cold night at home is as safe as before.

## Keys (§12)

22. In Settings → Controls, rebind one key in each group (On foot, General, Vehicles & machines,
    Hint panel) and use it. Try binding Move forward to Z: it's refused (Z turns the blower's chute
    at a walk-behind). Restore defaults puts everything back.
23. Rebind Inventory and Interact: the snowshoes toggle (hold Inventory, press Interact) follows.

## Fun Mode

24. No cold anywhere (outdoors at night, in the garage, in a cab).
25. The garage, the hints and the weather work the same as in Survival.
