# §17 — The hint panel

## What changed

- **The panel** (`scripts/ui/hint_panel.gd`, new, no `class_name`): a small panel at the top right
  (376 px wide, from y 150), on `game_root.time_layer` above the modals. A title, short pages,
  **Back** and **Next** (OK on the last page), and "[X] OK" under them. It never freezes the player,
  the world or the clock; a hint stays until OK; hints queue one at a time.
  - It waits (hidden) while the game is paused, asleep, dead or faded, and under the computer, the
    sleep prompt, Gerry's dialogue, the pause menu, F1 and the summary. It stays up, clickable, over
    the map, the TV, the letter and the photo (the owner's answer 2: the click path).
- **Keys** (`Settings.ACTIONS`): `hint_back` Left, `hint_next` Right, `hint_ok` X, in the new
  "hint" context (live on foot, in vehicles and at machines; it conflicts like a global action).
  `shares()` is now the one rule `rebind()` uses too (its inline copy had the same three contexts).
  Capped (`RATE_LIMITED`), read in `_input` only while a hint shows, never while typing. The
  Settings menu lists them under "Hint panel". No `CONTROLS_VERSION` bump.
- **The setting:** `gameplay/show_hints` (default on) in Settings → Gameplay → "Show hints". Off: no
  hint opens and the queue empties.
- **What goes through it:**
  - Gerry's how-to (`gerry_snow`): he says "Now, two ways to move snow." and the three pages open
    beside the speech box (the same wording). A re-talk during the job reopens it at page 1. It
    closes when the trail is done. With hints off he says all four lines, as before.
  - The first drive of each vehicle kind (the old 9 s panel, `Panels.vehicle_help`, removed): pages
    driving / the blade keys / back-drag for a plow, the blower or the bed for the others.
  - The first pickup of each kind of tool (the small shovel's notification sentence; the tool
    reminder's wording for the pusher, scraper, sleigh, electric shovel) and the blower's first grab
    (was a notification).
- **Seen hints:** `Game.hints_seen`, saved as `game.hints_seen` (version 3, optional), marked when a
  hint opens. Older saves derive them from the stage, the flags and the tools in hand
  (`Game.hints_from`).
- **Help:** the Help app has a new "Hints" section with every hint's content, keys bound now.
