# §7 — Weather you can see and hear

Visual and audio only (Decision 5): what falls, the wind chill and the forecasts are untouched, and
nothing reads the hours ahead. The audit (round 6's findings rechecked) is in STATUS.md under "§7".
How it works now: `docs/features/weather.md`.

## What changed

- **The falling snow** (`WeatherFX`, rewritten; `shaders/snow_flake.gdshader`, new): six-armed
  crystals in six shapes drawn in code (an atlas of 4 × 2 frames, no imported image), mixed sizes,
  fluttering and turning as they fall. More, faster and smaller as the snowfall gets heavier (a log
  scale of the visual rate, so heavy snow and a blizzard no longer look the same), slanted more as
  the wind rises. They start above every eave over a square 24 m across round the camera, moved
  upwind so the slant meets the camera. A one-shot fill puts a column's worth in the air at once on
  load, when the weather is forced and when the camera jumps (`preprocess` does nothing on this
  renderer).
- **Near the camera:** a few large soft flakes in light snow and calm air; never in the cab view.
- **Strong wind:** streaks driven almost sideways (from 30 km/h with snowfall); sheets of blowing
  snow along the ground and streams off roof ridges and bank tops from `blowing_snow_wind_kmh` (new,
  20 km/h) with snow on the ground — also in windy hours without snowfall; gusts (their own
  generator) come and go as pulses of denser, faster snow, the trees sway with them, and each starts
  a gust sound.
- **At night** the flakes are dimmed except near the street lamps and porch lanterns, where they
  glint.
- **Indoors:** flakes are no longer hidden when the camera is inside; the shader drops any inside a
  building footprint below its eaves (every `Buildings` footprint, the garage included, 47 now), so
  the snow is seen through windows and the open garage door and never inside.
- **Fog:** heavy 0.02 → 0.026 (the end of the street disappears), blizzard 0.05 → 0.065 (a near
  whiteout). The box outlines and the scoop rings ignore fog (`disable_fog`).
- **Trees** (`foliage`, `snow_soft`): a `sway` uniform (0 by default; the needles, bark and the snow
  on the boughs only) bends them in wind from 8 m/s, by height, with the gusts.
- **Sound** (`AudioMgr`): the weather loops follow the visual rate and wind (`weather_mix`) instead
  of the hour's condition: a soft hush for light snow (`snow_hush`, new), the strong wind rising
  through heavy snow, the blizzard's roar and a howl layer (`blizzard_howl`, new) in a gale. Gusts
  play one of four new variants (`wind_gust_1..4`) or the old whistle, timed by the visual gusts;
  the old timer and its three global random draws are gone. Rendered with `gen_audio.py` (numpy and
  scipy installed with the owner's OK, answer 11): only the six new files; no existing .wav changed.
- **F1:** "Windy (no snow)": 40 km/h and no snowfall for 2 game hours, the look and sound only
  (`Weather.debug_windy`; never saved). "Clear override" ends it.
- **Settings:** the particle density scales every layer (High as Medium, as before).
- **Shots and perf:** `shot.tscn -- --wind=kmh`; `perf.tscn -- --only=weather` (new scenarios).
- **Also:** `HouseBuilder.lantern_points()` (the lanterns' spot, shared with the glint);
  `perf.gd`'s heated-storm comment back above its function (§15 had moved it).

## Tested

- New, `--only=polish8 --test=test_s7_` (4, all pass):
  - `no_global_random`: every script in `scripts/` scanned (comments and strings left out); none
    calls the global `randf()` / `randi()` (or seeds it) more than before §7 (19 calls in 8 files then,
    16 now: the gust timer's 3 are gone); WeatherFX has its own generator.
  - `no_flake_inside`: the shader holds all 47 `Buildings` footprints (the player's garage included)
    up to their eaves and discards a particle inside one; the CPU mirror hides inside every footprint
    below its eaves and shows above it and just outside; in a blizzard, with the camera outdoors, in
    the house and in the garage (door open): the flakes start above every eave (10.6 m against 8.1 at
    most), every other layer's start points are outside every building, the snow still falls.
  - `levels_read_at_a_glance`: flakes light 1,247 < moderate 1,733 < heavy 2,390 < blizzard 5,000
    (Medium); near flakes in light and moderate snow, none from heavy; streaks from heavy, full in
    the blizzard; blowing snow in the blizzard, none in light snow's breeze; the trees' wind about 19 m/s in the blizzard against 4.2 in light snow (sway from 8); the
    needles, bark and bough snow sway, nothing else; F1's windy look (no flakes, wisps on the ground
    and off the roofs) with the weather's own wind, rate, wind chill and saved state unchanged;
    Low = 0.4 × Medium, High = Medium; the sound: the hush in light snow only, the strong wind rising
    light → moderate → heavy, the howl in the blizzard only, wind heard without snow.
  - `gusts_visual_and_audio_only`: in a blizzard, gusts come and go (peak > 0.3, calm frames
    between) and ask for a gust sound each; the hourly wind, the exposure and the weather unchanged.
