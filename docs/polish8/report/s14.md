# §14 — Cold nights, and a colder player

Survival Mode only. Fun Mode is unchanged. The audit (warmth before, the opening measured, time to
danger) is in STATUS.md under "§14: warmth before this round".

## What changed

- **A colder player** (`Survival.outdoor_loss_per_hour`): outdoors, the loss is (the weather's
  exposure + `player_base_loss_per_hour`) × the coat × the pouch × `player_cold_mult` × the night.
  - One multiplier couldn't bring every seed's opening near the target: Dec 1's weather differs by
    seed (wind chill −1 to −10 °C). The owner chose a base loss (answer 10). Tuned together: own
    loss 5 %/game h, ×2.2.
  - The opening (`contract_bench --job=opening`, 6.1–6.2 real minutes outdoors, done about 12:30 on
    Dec 1) now ends at 12.7–32.9 % on the five seeds (mean 22.3; `opening_target_warmth` 20). Before
    it ended at 85–94 %. No seed freezes.
  - Indoors, Gerry's house and his fire are unchanged (they read neither value).
- **Cold nights:** from dusk (`DayNight.sunset_hour`, as the sun sets) to 7:00 a.m., on foot
  outdoors (an open machine too), loss × `night_cold_mult` (1.5).
- **Cabs:** `VehicleData.heated_cabin` is the enclosed cab (reused, answer 6): every vehicle but the
  ATV.
  - Engine on: warms at `cab_warmth_per_hour` (50 %/game h, 40 per real minute; indoors 112) once
    the cab has warmed up. Before this, a cab warmed as fast as indoors.
  - Engine off: shelters (the air's exposure × the coat × the colder player; no wind, night or own
    loss).
  - The ATV and the walk-behind blowers: outdoors. The ATV's heated-cab upgrade stays unwired.
- **The engine switch** (new action `veh_engine`, I, vehicle context, capped; the vehicle hint's
  first page lists it): getting in starts the engine. Off, the vehicle doesn't drive, a running
  blower stops, the blade stays where it is (its keys do nothing), and the status and HUD lines say
  so.
- **The garage** (§15) shelters like an engine-off cab.
- **Hints:**
  - `cold_night`: "Cold night ahead. Sleep, or work from a heated cab." At dusk, if the forecast's low
    from dusk to 7:00 (`Weather.forecast_low`, the forecast model issued now) is under
    `cold_night_low_c` (−15 °C). Once a night. Not while indoors or in a running cab: then it shows
    the first time out that night. With hints off it is a warning notification.
  - `warm_up`: the first cold warning of a game opens how to warm up (indoors, a running cab, a
    pouch), once.
- **Balance** (data, defaults, generator; every rate's comment gives it as a felt rate):
  `player_cold_mult` 2.2, `player_base_loss_per_hour` 5, `night_cold_mult` 1.5,
  `cab_warmth_per_hour` 50, `cold_night_low_c` −15, `opening_target_warmth` 20.
- **The opening bench** (`contract_bench --job=opening --seed=n`): the bot plays the opening at the
  benches' human pace. It walks round the walkway's banks and the truck with a small route-finder;
  where it can't thread the banks (the last 29.5 m to Gerry), that stretch is timed at the game's
  wading pace. Its first version hung (the hand dig never reached the shovel); fixed.
- **Shot options:** `--cold_night=frames`, `--cab_warm=w` (`tests/shot.gd`).

## Tested

- New, `--only=polish8 --test=test_s14_` (10, all pass):
  - `opening_target`: each seed's measured opening, stepped on the clock: in 10–45 % (the warnings
    showing, not critical), never frozen, the mean within 5 of the target.
  - `indoors_unchanged`: home, half-heated home and Gerry's rates are the same with the new values at
    1 / 0 and as tuned.
  - `night_costs_more`: at the same wind chill, 10 p.m. and 6:30 a.m. cost × 1.5 the noon loss;
    7:15 a.m. and just before dusk cost the same as noon; just after dusk × 1.5.
  - `shelter`: a running pickup warms at `cab_warmth_per_hour` (less than indoors); the engine key
    stops it (sheltered loss, less than outdoors; it doesn't drive) and starts it again; the ATV and
    a walk-behind blower give no shelter; the garage shelters.
  - `cold_night_hint`: at dusk, once a night; indoors at dusk not then, out later that night it
    shows; in a running cab not; the low read is the forecast's (the same with the truth ahead
    scrambled); with hints off a notification.
  - `warm_up_hint_once`: two cold warnings, one hint.
  - `time_to_danger`: the table (STATUS.md); shorter at every temperature, shorter at night.
  - `path_job_survival`: a path job (1 real minute of work, a minute's walk each way) done on Dec 6
    before 9 p.m. with warm-up breaks indoors, never frozen.
  - `fun_and_sleep_unchanged`: Fun Mode never cools (outdoors at night, the garage); sleeping
    through a cold night at home (powered and in a power cut) ends the same as with the new values
    off.
  - `fast_forward_equal`: the night, the garage and a running cab give the same warmth at 1×, 2× and
    10×.
- Updated through balance lookups: `test_systems::test_warmth_rates` (the weather's share of the
  outdoor loss: ÷ the multipliers, − the own loss).
- Also run: see STATUS.md's §14 line.
