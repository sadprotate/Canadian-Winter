# §15 — The garage

## What changed

- **The shell** (`HouseBuilder`, the player's lot only; every other garage keeps its solid shell):
  - Hollow walls built inward from the old solid garage's faces (siding outside, so it looks as
    before; paint inside), the door opening framed by the old trim, a concrete floor, a ceiling, and
    the house wall's garage face painted.
  - A door from the house in its right wall (the kitchen/office area, clear of the furniture and of
    round 5's wall probes), two steps and a landing down to the garage floor (a ramp collider for
    the player; a block round them that only vehicles meet).
  - The overhead door's 4 sections are built closed here, so the menu shows a closed door.
- **The Garage node** (`scripts/world/garage.gd`, new, no `class_name`):
  - The overhead door runs up its tracks and under the ceiling in 2.4 s from its button (E) inside.
    Its collider fills the opening with its outer face on the old solid face (Z 100.5), so the blade
    stops where polish 7 measured; it rises with the door. It won't close on the player, a vehicle
    or a walk-behind in the doorway.
  - The rack: 6 slots (`garage_rack_slots`) on the house-side wall with the real tool models. F
    hangs the tool in your hands (inside the garage only), E takes it back or swaps it.
  - The ceiling light: on with the house's power, a real light only near the garage.
  - The first-visit hint (`garage`: the button, the rack).
- **The door from the house** (`game_root._build_doors`): a `Door`, locked until `flags.truck_keys`
  ("Locked: the keys are missing."); always open from the garage side. No save key.
- **Parking:** `Vehicle.fits_garage()`; vehicles that don't fit meet the doorway's gate and get
  "Too big for the garage." Getting out inside picks a spot where the player fits.
- **Sleep:** `send_truck_home()` leaves a truck that is in the garage; `_clear_place()` never moves a
  vehicle into the garage or the house.
- **The pad** lies on the garage floor (`Equipment.pad_garage_ok`); `Heating.sources()` skips it there.
- **Snow:** no change to the sim (the cells were already walls). The ground-snow mesh is hidden in
  the garage (`snow_hide_c`, a third shader global; the houses' two unchanged).
- **Climate:** `Player.in_garage` / `Survival.in_garage` (for §14). Inside, a vehicle gathers no roof
  snow and its snow melts off at `garage_snow_melt_per_hour` on the clock.
- **Saves:** `game.garage = {door_open, rack}` (version 3, optional, closed and empty by default).
- **Balance:** `garage_rack_slots` 6, `garage_snow_melt_per_hour` 0.3 (data, defaults, generator).
- **Perf:** `perf.tscn -- --only=garage` (inside, the truck parked, the door open, a storm's heaviest
  hour). A first run: avg 5.6 ms, p95 14.5 ms (the back-to-back comparison is in the final check).
